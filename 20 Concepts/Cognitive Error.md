## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Portfolio Management — Behavioral Biases:** Behavioural biases are grouped into cognitive errors and emotional biases. Cognitive errors arise from faulty reasoning or information processing and may be moderated with education; emotional biases stem from feelings and are often harder to correct.[^pm-l1-p27]
