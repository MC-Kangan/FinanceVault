# Investment Suitability

> [!summary]
> Base recommendations on updated client circumstances and judge investments in total-portfolio or mandate context.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p49][^eth-l1-p50]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Document experience, objectives, risk tolerance, and constraints in the investment policy statement; distinguish solicited and unsolicited trades.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p49][^eth-l1-p50]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p49][^eth-l1-p50]
