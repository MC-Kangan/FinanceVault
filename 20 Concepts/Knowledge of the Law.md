# Knowledge of the Law

> [!summary]
> Members and candidates must understand applicable requirements, follow the strictest applicable rule, and dissociate from continuing violations.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p12][^eth-l1-p14]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Do not knowingly participate in or assist violations. Escalate, seek advice, and dissociate completely if misconduct continues.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p12][^eth-l1-p14]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p12][^eth-l1-p14]
