# Ethics and Trust

> [!summary]
> Ethics applies principles of conduct beyond minimum legal compliance and supports trust in investment professionals and capital markets.[^eth-l1-p5]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

The Code states broad ethical principles, while enforceable Standards specify required professional conduct. Ethical conduct may demand more than legal compliance.[^eth-l1-p5]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-l1-p5]
