## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Portfolio Management — Modern Portfolio Theory and Asset Pricing:** The capital allocation line combines a risk-free asset and one risky asset. Visually verified: $E(R_p)=r_f+[(R_A-r_f)/\sigma_A]\sigma_p$; the slope is the risky asset’s Sharpe ratio, and the highest-slope feasible CAL is preferred.[^pm-l1-p8]

**Portfolio Management — Modern Portfolio Theory and Asset Pricing:** Visually verified performance measures: Jensen’s $\alpha_p=R_p-[r_f+\beta_p(E(R_M)-r_f)]$; Treynor $(R_p-r_f)/\beta_p$; $M^2=(R_p-r_f)\sigma_M/\sigma_p+r_f$; $M^2$ alpha $=M^2-R_M$; Sharpe $(R_p-r_f)/\sigma_p$.[^pm-l1-p14]
