# Lin-Log Model

> [!summary]
> A lin-log model relates the level of the dependent variable to the logarithm of the independent variable.[^quant-l1-p59]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A lin-log model relates the level of the dependent variable to the logarithm of the independent variable.[^quant-l1-p59]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Simple Linear Regression]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p59]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p59]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Simple Linear Regression]]. Compare it with [[Log-Lin Model]], [[Log-Log Model]].
