## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Central banks issue currency, support payments and banks, supervise finance, act as lender of last resort, and pursue monetary objectives.[^eco-l1-p33]

### Interpretation

Identify Central Bank within its market, cycle, policy, geopolitical, trade, or exchange-rate framework and distinguish its direction and decision use from adjacent concepts.[^eco-l1-p33]
