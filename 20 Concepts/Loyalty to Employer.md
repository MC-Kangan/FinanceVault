# Loyalty to Employer

> [!summary]
> In employment matters, act for the employer and do not deprive it of skills, disclose confidential information, or cause harm.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p58][^eth-l1-p59][^eth-l1-p61]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Preparation to depart may be allowed, but competition before departure is not. Distinguish employer records from general knowledge and skills; client-protective whistleblowing may be consistent with loyalty.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p58][^eth-l1-p59][^eth-l1-p61]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p58][^eth-l1-p59][^eth-l1-p61]
