# Professional Conduct Program

> [!summary]
> The Professional Conduct Program investigates potential Code and Standards violations and administers the CFA Institute disciplinary process.[^eth-l1-p6]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

The process involves investigation, possible cautionary or disciplinary outcomes, and review through CFA Institute procedures.[^eth-l1-p6]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-l1-p6]
