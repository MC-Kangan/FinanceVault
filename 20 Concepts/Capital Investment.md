# Capital Investment

> [!summary]
> Capital Investment is a Level 1 concept in [[Corporate Issuers]]; its separate note preserves a reusable definition and a clear destination for later-level extensions.[^ci-l1-p24]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Capital projects support maintenance, compliance, efficiency, risk management, or growth, with risk generally rising as projects move beyond the core business.[^ci-l1-p24]

### Interpretation

Identify the role of Capital Investment within its contract, decision rule, governance mechanism, financing choice, or operating framework, and distinguish it from adjacent concepts.[^ci-l1-p24]

<!-- enrichment:corporate-issuers:start -->

### Slide-level enrichment

Business-maintenance investment includes going-concern projects that continue operations, improve efficiency, or manage risk, plus regulatory/compliance projects. Growth investment includes expansion within the existing business, normally low-to-moderate risk, and new lines outside the core business, normally higher risk. R&D and acquisitions can fall in either category depending on whether they remain within the core business.[^ci-l1-p24]
