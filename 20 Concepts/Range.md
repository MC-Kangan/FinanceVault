# Range

> [!summary]
> Range is the maximum observation minus the minimum observation.[^quant-l1-p16]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

The core relationship is $Range=x_{max}-x_{min}$.[^quant-l1-p16]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p16]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p16]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]]. Compare it with [[Box-and-Whisker Plot]], [[Mean Absolute Deviation]].
