## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Financial Markets and Trading:** Quote-driven markets use dealer quotes; brokered markets use search, especially for unique/large orders; order-driven markets match public orders by rules. Priority may reflect price, display, and time; pricing can be uniform or discriminatory.[^eq-l1-p10]

### Equity slide enrichment

Quote-driven or dealer markets are commonly OTC. Brokered markets suit unique or illiquid instruments. Order-driven markets match submitted orders using formal rules rather than dealer inventory.[^eq-enrich-p9][^eq-enrich-p10]
