# Standard Error

> [!summary]
> Standard error measures the dispersion of a statistic's sampling distribution.[^quant-l1-p40]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

The core relationship is $s_{\bar{x}}=\dfrac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$.[^quant-l1-p40]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Sampling and Inference]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p40]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p40]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Sampling and Inference]]. Compare it with [[Central Limit Theorem]], [[Hypothesis Testing]].
