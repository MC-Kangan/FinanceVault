# Free Cash Flow to Equity

> [!summary]
> Free cash flow to equity (FCFE) is cash available to common equity after debt financing costs and net borrowing are reflected.[^fsa-l1-p40]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula from FCFF

$$
\text{FCFE}=\text{FCFF}-\text{Interest}(1-t)+\text{Net Borrowing}
$$
[^fsa-l1-p40]

### Formula from cash flow from operations

$$
\text{FCFE}=\text{CFO}-\text{FCInv}+\text{Net Borrowing}
$$
[^fsa-l1-p40]

### Interpretation

Subtracting after-tax interest removes the debt holders' financing claim, while net borrowing adds cash raised from creditors net of debt repayment.[^fsa-l1-p40]

### Common pitfalls

Net borrowing can be negative when debt repayment exceeds new borrowing, reducing cash available to equity holders.[^fsa-l1-p40]
