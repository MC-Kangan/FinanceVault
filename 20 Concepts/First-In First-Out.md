# First-In First-Out

> [!summary]
> FIFO assumes the earliest costs enter cost of goods sold first; during rising prices it produces lower COGS and higher ending inventory than LIFO.[^fsa-l1-p50]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics
