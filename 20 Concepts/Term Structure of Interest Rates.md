# Term Structure of Interest Rates

> [!summary]
> Distinguish the spot, par, and forward curves. Under normal conditions the forward curve typically lies above the spot curve and the par curve below it; flat and inverted shapes can alter or eliminate that ordering.[^fi-valuation-p54]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

- Distinguish the spot, par, and forward curves. Under normal conditions the forward curve typically lies above the spot curve and the par curve below it; flat and inverted shapes can alter or eliminate that ordering.[^fi-valuation-p54]

![[source-fi-l1-speed-review-p054-yield-curves.png]][^fi-valuation-p54]

Figure: The relative positions of spot, par, and forward curves depend on the shape of the term structure; the chart contrasts normal, flat, and inverted examples.[^fi-valuation-p54]

### Exam focus

Identify the definition, direction of the principal relationships, and the financial-statement or valuation effect tested by this concept.[^fi-valuation-p54]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

Spot rates discount individual cash flows; the par rate makes a bond price equal par; forward rates are future-period rates implied by current spot rates. Spot, par, and forward curves can be upward sloping, flat, or inverted.[^fi-valuation-p52][^fi-valuation-p53][^fi-valuation-p54]
