## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Public and private companies differ in exchange listing, liquidity, price transparency, disclosure, ownership overlap, and share issuance.[^ci-l1-p5]

### Interpretation

<!-- enrichment:corporate-issuers:start -->

### Slide-level enrichment

Public shares trade on an exchange, are easier to transfer, and have price transparency; public issuers face registration plus financial and non-financial disclosure. Private shares are harder to transfer, owner-manager overlap is more likely, disclosure is mainly non-financial, and issuance commonly occurs through private placements to accredited investors.[^ci-l1-p5]
