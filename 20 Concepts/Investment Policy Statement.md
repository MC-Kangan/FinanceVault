### Formula and mechanics

**Portfolio Management — Portfolio Management Process and IPS:** An investment policy statement records governance, client circumstances, return objectives, risk objectives, and constraints. The constraint mnemonic is TTLLU: time horizon, taxes, liquidity, legal/regulatory, and unique circumstances.[^pm-l1-p22]
