### Slide-level enrichment

The audit committee monitors financial reporting, supervises internal audit, and recommends external auditors. The remuneration committee proposes director and executive compensation with shareholder say-on-pay. The nomination/governance committee evaluates candidates, oversees board elections, and supports governance policies. Risk and investment committees are additional specialist committees; independence is identified as best practice.[^ci-l1-p11]
