# Student's t-Distribution

> [!summary]
> Student's t-distribution is symmetric with heavier tails than the normal distribution and depends on degrees of freedom.[^quant-l1-p30]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Student's t-distribution is symmetric with heavier tails than the normal distribution and depends on degrees of freedom.[^quant-l1-p30]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Probability Foundations]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p30]
