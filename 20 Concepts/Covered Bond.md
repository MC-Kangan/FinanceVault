## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

- Covered bonds are issued by a bank and collateralized by a ring-fenced cover pool that remains on the bank balance sheet, so they are not full securitizations through an independent SPV.[^fi-structured-p94]
- Investors have dual recourse to the cover pool and the issuer's unencumbered assets. Cover pools are monitored and usually overcollateralized; redemption regimes include hard bullet, soft bullet, and conditional pass-through.[^fi-structured-p95]

### Exam focus

Identify the definition, direction of the principal relationships, and the financial-statement or valuation effect tested by this concept.[^fi-structured-p94]

### Alternative Investments context

Covered bonds provide public debt exposure backed by a ring-fenced cover pool while retaining issuer recourse.[^alt-l1-p24]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

Covered bonds keep a ring-fenced cover pool on the issuer's balance sheet and give investors dual recourse to pool and issuer. The pool is dynamically monitored and often overcollateralized; repayment regimes include hard bullet, soft bullet, and conditional pass-through.[^fi-structured-p94][^fi-structured-p95]
