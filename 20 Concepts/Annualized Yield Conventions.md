# Annualized Yield Conventions

> [!summary]
> Separate the rate per period, stated annual yield or APR, and effective annual rate.[^fi-valuation-p45]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

- Separate the rate per period, stated annual yield or APR, and effective annual rate.[^fi-valuation-p45]

$$
r=\frac{R_s}{n}
$$
[^fi-valuation-p45]

$$
EAR=(1+r)^n-1=\left(1+\frac{R_s}{n}\right)^n-1
$$
[^fi-valuation-p45]

$$
\left(1+\frac{APR_m}{m}\right)^m=\left(1+\frac{APR_n}{n}\right)^n
$$
[^fi-valuation-p45]

### Exam focus

Identify the definition, direction of the principal relationships, and the financial-statement or valuation effect tested by this concept.[^fi-valuation-p45]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

Current yield equals annual coupon divided by flat price and omits time value, accrued interest, and capital gain or loss. Simple yield adds straight-line annual amortization of discount or premium before dividing by flat price.[^fi-valuation-p45][^fi-valuation-p46]

$$EAR=\left(1+\frac{R_s}{n}\right)^n-1$$
[^fi-valuation-p45]
