# Interest Coverage Ratio

> [!summary]
> The interest coverage ratio compares operating earnings with interest obligations and is used as a solvency and corporate-credit indicator.[^fsa-l1-p45][^fi-l1-p87]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula

$$
\text{Interest Coverage Ratio}=\frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Interest Payments or Expense}}
$$
[^fsa-l1-p45][^fi-l1-p87]

The FSA slide labels the denominator interest payments, while the corporate-credit slide expresses the same coverage idea as operating income divided by interest expense.[^fsa-l1-p45][^fi-l1-p87]

### Interpretation

A higher ratio means a larger operating-earnings cushion relative to interest obligations; weaker coverage increases concern about debt-service capacity.[^fi-l1-p87]

### Common pitfalls

Check the exact numerator and denominator definitions before comparing companies or periods. Accounting classifications and non-recurring operating items can affect reported EBIT and interest expense.[^fi-l1-p87]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

Corporate credit analysis combines business model, competition, governance, and business risk with profitability, coverage, leverage, and cash-flow measures. These accounting-derived measures are genuine bridges between Financial Statement Analysis and Fixed Income.[^fi-credit-p87]
