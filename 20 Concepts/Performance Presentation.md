# Performance Presentation

> [!summary]
> Make reasonable efforts to present investment performance fairly, accurately, and completely.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p52][^eth-l1-p53]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Do not present a selected account as the entire firm's result; make supporting information available and avoid claims that imply guaranteed future performance.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p52][^eth-l1-p53]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p52][^eth-l1-p53]
