# Z-Score

> [!summary]
> A z-score expresses an observation's distance from the mean in standard-deviation units.[^quant-l1-p28]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

The core relationship is $z=\dfrac{x-\mu}{\sigma}$.[^quant-l1-p28]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Probability Foundations]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p28]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p28]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Probability Foundations]]. Compare it with [[Standard Normal Distribution]], [[Shortfall Risk]].
