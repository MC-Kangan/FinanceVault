# Last-In First-Out

> [!summary]
> LIFO assumes the latest costs enter cost of goods sold first; during rising prices it produces higher COGS and lower ending inventory than FIFO and is not permitted under IFRS.[^fsa-l1-p50]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

LIFO assumes the latest costs enter cost of goods sold first; during rising prices it produces higher COGS and lower ending inventory than FIFO and is not permitted under IFRS.[^fsa-l1-p50]
