# Reference to CFA Institute the CFA Designation and the CFA Program

> [!summary]
> Do not misrepresent or exaggerate the meaning or implications of CFA Institute membership, the CFA designation, or candidacy.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p91][^eth-l1-p92][^eth-l1-p93]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Use CFA as an adjective rather than a noun, avoid performance guarantees, and describe membership, charterholder status, and candidacy accurately.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p91][^eth-l1-p92][^eth-l1-p93]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p91][^eth-l1-p92][^eth-l1-p93]
