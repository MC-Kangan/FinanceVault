# Communication with Clients and Prospective Clients

> [!summary]
> Disclose services and costs, explain investment processes and material changes, communicate important risks and limitations, and distinguish fact from opinion.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p73][^eth-l1-p74]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Communications should identify material decision factors and remain complete, timely, and understandable to clients and prospects.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p73][^eth-l1-p74]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p73][^eth-l1-p74]
