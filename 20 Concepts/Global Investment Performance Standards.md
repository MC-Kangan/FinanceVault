# Global Investment Performance Standards

> [!summary]
> GIPS are voluntary ethical standards for fair representation and full disclosure of investment performance.[^eth-l1-p95]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Compliance is claimed firm-wide rather than for a single fund or product. Firms follow applicable law and address conflicts between law and GIPS as required.[^eth-l1-p95]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-l1-p95]
