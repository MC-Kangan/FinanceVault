# One-Tailed and Two-Tailed Tests

> [!summary]
> A one-tailed test specifies a directional alternative; a two-tailed test permits deviations in either direction.[^quant-l1-p41]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A one-tailed test specifies a directional alternative; a two-tailed test permits deviations in either direction.[^quant-l1-p41]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Sampling and Inference]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p41]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p41]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Sampling and Inference]]. Compare it with [[Null and Alternative Hypotheses]], [[Significance Level]].
