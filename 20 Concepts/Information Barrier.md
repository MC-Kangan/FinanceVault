# Information Barrier

> [!summary]
> Information barriers restrict material nonpublic information within a firm and support compliant research, trading, and client activity.[^eth-l1-p34]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Controls should govern access, communications, proprietary trading, market making, and customer facilitation when sensitive information is present.[^eth-l1-p34]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-l1-p34]
