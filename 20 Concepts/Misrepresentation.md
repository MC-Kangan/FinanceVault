# Misrepresentation

> [!summary]
> Do not knowingly misrepresent investment analysis, recommendations, actions, qualifications, services, models, benchmarks, or performance.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p21][^eth-l1-p23][^eth-l1-p25]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Attribute others' work, including material from prior employers, and use reasonable care with third-party research and outsourced services.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p21][^eth-l1-p23][^eth-l1-p25]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p21][^eth-l1-p23][^eth-l1-p25]
