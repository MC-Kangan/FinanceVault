# Intangible Asset

> [!summary]
> An intangible asset is an identifiable nonmonetary asset without physical substance; finite-life assets are amortized and indefinite-life assets are tested for impairment.[^fsa-l1-p29]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

An intangible asset is an identifiable nonmonetary asset without physical substance; finite-life assets are amortized and indefinite-life assets are tested for impairment.[^fsa-l1-p29]

<!-- enrichment:fsa:start -->
### Recognition and subsequent measurement

Intangibles may be purchased, internally generated, or acquired in a business combination. Finite-lived assets are amortized systematically, reviewed for changes in useful life, and tested for impairment; indefinite-lived assets are not amortized and are tested at least annually.[^fsa-detail-p29]

Research is expensed. The source permits IFRS development-cost capitalization after the stated criteria are met and limits US-GAAP capitalization largely to qualifying software development.[^fsa-detail-p54]
