# Responsibilities of Supervisors

> [!summary]
> Supervisors must make reasonable efforts to ensure persons under their authority comply with law and the Code and Standards.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p66][^eth-l1-p67]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Establish adequate compliance systems, investigate warning signs, and take corrective action; instructions or disclaimers alone are insufficient.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p66][^eth-l1-p67]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p66][^eth-l1-p67]
