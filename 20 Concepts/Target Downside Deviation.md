# Target Downside Deviation

> [!summary]
> Target downside deviation measures dispersion only for observations below a chosen target.[^quant-l1-p16]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Target downside deviation measures dispersion only for observations below a chosen target.[^quant-l1-p16]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p16]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p16]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]]. Compare it with [[Standard Deviation]], [[Coefficient of Variation]].
