# Conditional Variance

> [!summary]
> Conditional variance measures dispersion under a stated event or information set.[^quant-l1-p25]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Conditional variance measures dispersion under a stated event or information set.[^quant-l1-p25]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Probability Foundations]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p25]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p25]
