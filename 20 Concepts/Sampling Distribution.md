# Sampling Distribution

> [!summary]
> A sampling distribution is the distribution of a statistic across all possible samples of a fixed size.[^quant-l1-p34]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A sampling distribution is the distribution of a statistic across all possible samples of a fixed size.[^quant-l1-p34]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Sampling and Inference]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p34]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p34]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Sampling and Inference]]. Compare it with [[Sampling Error]], [[Simple Random Sampling]].
