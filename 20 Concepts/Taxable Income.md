# Taxable Income

> [!summary]
> Taxable income is taxable revenue less deductible expense; tax payable is taxable income multiplied by the applicable tax rate and represents a current tax obligation and eventual cash outflow.[^fsa-tax-p73]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Taxable income is taxable revenue less deductible expense; tax payable is taxable income multiplied by the applicable tax rate and represents a current tax obligation and eventual cash outflow.[^fsa-tax-p73]

$$
\text{Taxable income}=\text{Taxable revenue}-\text{Deductible expense}
$$
[^fsa-tax-p73]

$$
\text{Tax payable}=\text{Taxable income}\times\text{Tax rate}
$$
[^fsa-tax-p73]

<!-- enrichment:fsa:start -->
### Tax reconciliation

$$
Income\ tax\ expense=Current\ tax\ payable+\Delta DTL-\Delta DTA
$$[^fsa-detail-p73]

Taxable income determines current tax payable, while pretax accounting income is the financial-reporting measure used in the net-income bridge.[^fsa-detail-p73]
