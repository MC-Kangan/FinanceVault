## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Portfolio Management — Modern Portfolio Theory and Asset Pricing:** Systematic risk affects the market and cannot be diversified away; unsystematic risk can. Visually verified: $\beta_i=\operatorname{Cov}(R_i,R_M)/\sigma_M^2=\rho_{iM}\sigma_i/\sigma_M$ and $\beta_p=\sum_iw_i\beta_i$. Only systematic risk is priced in the model.[^pm-l1-p10]
