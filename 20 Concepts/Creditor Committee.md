### Slide-level enrichment

Creditor governance uses bond indentures and creditor or ad hoc committees. Employee mechanisms include labor law and employment contracts; customer and supplier mechanisms rely on contracts; government mechanisms include law, regulation, social-media scrutiny, and governance codes. Poor governance can cause weak controls, legal/regulatory/reputational harm, greater default or bankruptcy risk, and rejection of value-creating investments.[^ci-l1-p12]
