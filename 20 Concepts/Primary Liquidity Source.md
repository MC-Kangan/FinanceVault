# Primary Liquidity Source

> [!summary]
> Primary Liquidity Source is a Level 1 concept in [[Corporate Issuers]]; its separate note preserves a reusable definition and a clear destination for later-level extensions.[^ci-l1-p16]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Primary liquidity comes from readily available operating and financing sources; secondary sources involve more consequential actions such as asset sales or restructuring.[^ci-l1-p16]

### Interpretation

Identify the role of Primary Liquidity Source within its contract, decision rule, governance mechanism, financing choice, or operating framework, and distinguish it from adjacent concepts.[^ci-l1-p16]

### Slide-level enrichment

Primary liquidity comes from cash and marketable securities that can be sold without significant value loss, borrowing or supplier trade credit, CFO, and free cash flow. The source uses $Free\ cash\ flow=CFO-Investments\ in\ long\text{-}term\ assets$. Secondary actions include cutting dividends or capital expenditure, issuing equity, renegotiating contracts, selling assets, or restructuring; these can dilute owners, sacrifice opportunities, impair long-term value, and create liquidation costs.[^ci-l1-p16]
