# Asset-Backed Token

> [!summary]
> An asset-backed token is a digital claim on a physical or financial asset and can permit fractional ownership.[^alt-l1-p45]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

An asset-backed token is a digital claim on a physical or financial asset and can permit fractional ownership.[^alt-l1-p45]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Digital Assets]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p45]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p45]
