# Forward Rate Agreement

> [!summary]
> Forward Rate Agreement is a Level 1 concept in [[Derivatives]]; its separate note preserves a reusable definition and a clear destination for later-level extensions.[^der-l1-p22]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

An FRA fixes a future borrowing or lending rate on a notional loan and is quoted by contract-expiry and loan-end months.[^der-l1-p22]

### Interpretation
