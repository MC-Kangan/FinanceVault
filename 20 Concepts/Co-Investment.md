# Co-Investment

> [!summary]
> Co-investment combines an indirect fund commitment with rights to invest directly alongside the fund in selected assets.[^alt-l1-p5]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Co-investment combines an indirect fund commitment with rights to invest directly alongside the fund in selected assets.[^alt-l1-p5]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Foundations and Structures]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p5]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p5]
