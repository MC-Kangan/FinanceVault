## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Agency conflicts arise among managers, directors, controlling and minority shareholders, and creditors because incentives and payoff structures differ.[^ci-l1-p9]

### Slide-level enrichment

The source separates three agency conflicts. Shareholders versus management or directors can produce entrenchment, empire building, inappropriate risk appetite, self-dealing, and insufficient effort; heavy stock incentives may encourage excessive risk-taking, while little stock compensation may encourage risk aversion. Controlling versus minority shareholder conflicts intensify with concentrated ownership and dual-class shares. Shareholders and creditors prefer different risk because their payoff structures differ.[^ci-l1-p9]
