# Survivorship Bias

> [!summary]
> Survivorship bias overstates performance when failed or closed funds disappear from the observed sample.[^alt-l1-p38]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Survivorship bias overstates performance when failed or closed funds disappear from the observed sample.[^alt-l1-p38]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Hedge Funds]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p38]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p38]
