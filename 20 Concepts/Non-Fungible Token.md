> [!summary]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A non-fungible token represents a unique digital item or ownership claim.[^alt-l1-p42]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Digital Assets]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p42]
