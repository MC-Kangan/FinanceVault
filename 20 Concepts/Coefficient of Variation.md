# Coefficient of Variation

> [!summary]
> The coefficient of variation measures dispersion per unit of mean.[^quant-l1-p17]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

The core relationship is $CV=\dfrac{s}{\bar{x}}$.[^quant-l1-p17]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p17]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p17]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]]. Compare it with [[Target Downside Deviation]], [[Scatter Plot]].
