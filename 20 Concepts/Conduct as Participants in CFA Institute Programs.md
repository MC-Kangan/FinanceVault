# Conduct as Participants in CFA Institute Programs

> [!summary]
> Do not compromise the reputation, integrity, validity, or security of CFA Institute programs.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p88][^eth-l1-p89]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Protect confidential exam information and follow program rules; discussion of curriculum facts and general exam experience is distinct from revealing protected content.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p88][^eth-l1-p89]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p88][^eth-l1-p89]
