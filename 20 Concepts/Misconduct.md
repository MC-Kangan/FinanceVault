# Misconduct

> [!summary]
> Do not engage in dishonesty, fraud, deceit, or conduct that reflects adversely on professional reputation, integrity, or competence.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p27][^eth-l1-p28]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Purely personal conduct is distinguished from behavior with a professional nexus or consequences for integrity and competence.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p27][^eth-l1-p28]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p27][^eth-l1-p28]
