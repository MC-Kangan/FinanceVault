# Commercial Paper

> [!summary]
> Commercial paper is a short-term unsecured promissory note used by companies and banks, with rollover risk because repayment commonly depends on issuing new paper.[^fi-markets-p25][^fi-markets-p28]

## Level 1 - Foundation

Nonfinancial companies use commercial paper for short-term funding; banks may also issue it. Asset-backed commercial paper is issued by a bank-sponsored special-purpose entity and is supported by the bank as sponsor and backup credit provider.[^fi-markets-p25][^fi-markets-p28]

Commercial paper is generally unsecured and exposed to rollover risk. Its money-market return is commonly quoted on a discount-rate basis.[^fi-markets-p25][^fi-valuation-p51]
