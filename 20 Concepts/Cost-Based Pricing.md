### Slide-level enrichment

A business model answers who the customers are, what is offered, where and through which channels it reaches them, and how much/how customers pay. Channels include traditional distribution, direct sales versus agency, and omnichannel. Pricing structures include value- or cost-based pricing, tiered and dynamic pricing, auctions, bundling, add-ons, razor-and-blades, penetration, freemium, hidden revenue, subscriptions, leasing, licensing, franchising, and fractionalization.[^ci-l1-p42]
