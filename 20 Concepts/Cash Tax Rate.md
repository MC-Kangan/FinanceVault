# Cash Tax Rate

> [!summary]
> The cash tax rate relates cash taxes paid to pretax accounting income and is useful for cash-flow forecasting.[^fsa-tax-p79]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

The cash tax rate relates cash taxes paid to pretax accounting income and is useful for cash-flow forecasting.[^fsa-tax-p79]

$$
\text{Cash tax rate}=\frac{\text{Cash taxes paid}}{\text{Pretax accounting income}}
$$
[^fsa-tax-p79]

<!-- enrichment:fsa:start -->
### Forecasting measures

$$
Effective\ tax\ rate=\frac{Income\ tax\ expense}{Pretax\ income}
$$[^fsa-detail-p79]

$$
Cash\ tax\ rate=\frac{Cash\ taxes\ paid}{Pretax\ income}
$$[^fsa-detail-p79]

The source positions the effective rate for earnings forecasts and the cash rate for cash-flow forecasts.[^fsa-detail-p79]
<!-- enrichment:fsa:end -->
