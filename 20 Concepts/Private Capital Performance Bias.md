# Private Capital Performance Bias

> [!summary]
> Private-capital performance measures can understate risk because of survivorship, backfill, valuation smoothing, and limited mark-to-market data.[^alt-l1-p21]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Private-capital performance measures can understate risk because of survivorship, backfill, valuation smoothing, and limited mark-to-market data.[^alt-l1-p21]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Private Capital]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p21]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p21]
