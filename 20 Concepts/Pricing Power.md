## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Industry and Company Analysis:** Pricing power is the ability to raise price without proportionate demand loss; drivers include differentiation, switching costs, substitutes, customer concentration, and rivalry.[^eq-l1-p47]

### Equity slide enrichment

Pricing power is the ability to set prices and other terms without reducing volume. It is supported by differentiation, entry barriers, few economical substitutes, switching costs, and retention, and weakened by commoditization, ready substitutes, and low loyalty. Assess it through profit margins as well as observed prices.[^eq-enrich-p47]

<!-- enrichment:equity:end -->

<!-- enrichment:corporate-issuers:start -->

### Slide-level enrichment

A business model answers who the customers are, what is offered, where and through which channels it reaches them, and how much/how customers pay. Channels include traditional distribution, direct sales versus agency, and omnichannel. Pricing structures include value- or cost-based pricing, tiered and dynamic pricing, auctions, bundling, add-ons, razor-and-blades, penetration, freemium, hidden revenue, subscriptions, leasing, licensing, franchising, and fractionalization.[^ci-l1-p42]
