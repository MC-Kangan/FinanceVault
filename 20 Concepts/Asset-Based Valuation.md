## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Equity Valuation:** Valuation families: present value (DDM, FCFE, FCFF, residual income), market multiples (P/E, P/B, P/S, P/CF, EV multiples), and asset-based valuation.[^eq-l1-p71]

**Equity Investments — Equity Valuation:** $EV/EBITDA$ uses $EV=$ common equity + preferred equity + debt − cash/equivalents and aids comparison across capital structures. Asset-based equity value is market assets minus liabilities; it suits tangible/readily valued or liquidating firms.[^eq-l1-p76]

<!-- enrichment:equity:start -->

### Equity slide enrichment

Asset-based equity value is $Market\ value\ of\ assets-Market\ value\ of\ liabilities$. It suits tangible short-term assets, readily valued assets, private firms, and liquidation. It is weak when market values are hard to determine, book and fair values diverge, it understates going-concern value, or hyperinflation distorts measurement.[^eq-enrich-p76]
