# Record Retention

> [!summary]
> Develop and maintain records supporting analysis, recommendations, actions, and investment-related client communications.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p76][^eth-l1-p77]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Employer-created records generally belong to the employer; preserve relevant electronic and social-media records under applicable retention requirements.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p76][^eth-l1-p77]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p76][^eth-l1-p77]
