# Coefficient of Determination

> [!summary]
> The coefficient of determination is the proportion of dependent-variable variation explained by the regression.[^quant-l1-p56]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

The core relationship is $R^2=\dfrac{SSR}{SST}=1-\dfrac{SSE}{SST}$.[^quant-l1-p56]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Simple Linear Regression]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p56]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p56]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Simple Linear Regression]]. Compare it with [[Mean Squared Error]], [[F-Statistic]].
