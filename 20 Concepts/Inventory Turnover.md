# Inventory Turnover

> [!summary]
> Inventory turnover relates cost of goods sold to average inventory and measures inventory movement efficiency.[^fsa-l1-p44]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Inventory turnover relates cost of goods sold to average inventory and measures inventory movement efficiency.[^fsa-l1-p44]

<!-- enrichment:fsa:start -->
### Formula

$$
Inventory\ turnover=\frac{COGS}{Average\ inventory}
$$[^fsa-detail-p44]
