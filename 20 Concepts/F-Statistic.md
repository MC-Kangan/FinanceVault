# F-Statistic

> [!summary]
> The regression F-statistic compares explained variation with unexplained variation.[^quant-l1-p56]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

The core relationship is $F=\dfrac{MSR}{MSE}$.[^quant-l1-p56]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Simple Linear Regression]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p56]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p56]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Simple Linear Regression]]. Compare it with [[Coefficient of Determination]], [[Standard Error of Estimate]].
