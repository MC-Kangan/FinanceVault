# Debt-to-EBITDA Ratio

> [!summary]
> Debt-to-EBITDA is a leverage measure used in corporate credit analysis to compare debt with an earnings-based proxy for operating cash-generating capacity.[^fi-l1-p87]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula

$$
\text{Debt-to-EBITDA}=\frac{\text{Debt}}{\text{EBITDA}}
$$
[^fi-l1-p87]

### Interpretation

Holding other factors constant, more debt relative to EBITDA indicates greater leverage and typically weaker capacity to service debt from operating performance.[^fi-l1-p87]

### Common pitfalls

EBITDA is not free cash flow: it omits working-capital investment, fixed-capital investment, taxes, and financing cash flows.[^fi-l1-p87]
