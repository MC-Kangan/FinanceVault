## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Portfolio Management — Modern Portfolio Theory and Asset Pricing:** Visually verified utility: $U=E(R)-0.5A\sigma^2$, with $A>0$ risk-averse, $A=0$ neutral, and $A<0$ seeking. Indifference curves contain equal-utility risk/return pairs; for risk-averse investors they slope upward and are convex, with curvature increasing in risk aversion.[^pm-l1-p5]
