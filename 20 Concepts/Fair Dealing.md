# Fair Dealing

> [!summary]
> Deal fairly and objectively with clients when providing analysis, recommendations, investment action, or other professional services.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p45][^eth-l1-p46][^eth-l1-p47]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Fair treatment does not require identical service. Disclosed service tiers may differ, while timing and allocations must not unfairly disadvantage clients.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p45][^eth-l1-p46][^eth-l1-p47]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p45][^eth-l1-p46][^eth-l1-p47]
