## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Equity Valuation:** Visually verified: $V_0=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}D_i/(1+r)^i$; Gordon $V_0=D_1/(r-g)$ with constant $g<r$, and $g=(1-\text{payout})ROE$. Two-stage DDM adds high-growth dividends and terminal value; preferred stock $V_0=D/r$.[^eq-l1-p73]

### Equity slide enrichment

For constant perpetual growth:[^eq-enrich-p73]

$$
V_0=\frac{D_1}{r-g},\qquad g=Retention\ rate\times ROE=(1-Payout\ ratio)\times ROE
$$[^eq-enrich-p73]

The model assumes constant perpetual dividend growth and $g<r$.[^eq-enrich-p73]
