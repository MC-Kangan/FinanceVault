# Present Value Factor

> [!summary]
> A present value factor discounts a future amount back to today.[^quant-l1-p7]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A present value factor discounts a future amount back to today.[^quant-l1-p7]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Interest Rates and Investment Returns]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p7]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p7]
