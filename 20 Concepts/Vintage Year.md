# Vintage Year

> [!summary]
> Vintage year is the year a private fund makes its first investment and can materially affect realized performance.[^alt-l1-p22]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Vintage year is the year a private fund makes its first investment and can materially affect realized performance.[^alt-l1-p22]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Private Capital]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p22]

### Common pitfalls
