> [!summary]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

A forward commitment's price is the fixed contractual term, while its value changes after initiation and begins at zero for an at-market contract.[^der-l1-p19]

### Interpretation

Identify the role of Derivative Price within its contract, decision rule, governance mechanism, financing choice, or operating framework, and distinguish it from adjacent concepts.[^der-l1-p19]
