# Recapitalization

> [!summary]
> A recapitalization changes the capital structure, sometimes adding debt to fund a distribution to equity holders.[^alt-l1-p19]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A recapitalization changes the capital structure, sometimes adding debt to fund a distribution to equity holders.[^alt-l1-p19]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Private Capital]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p19]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p19]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Alternative Investments]] through [[Alternatives - Private Capital]]. Compare it with [[Secondary Sale]], [[Private Debt]].
