# Lognormal Distribution

> [!summary]
> A lognormally distributed variable is positive and has a natural logarithm that is normally distributed.[^quant-l1-p29]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A lognormally distributed variable is positive and has a natural logarithm that is normally distributed.[^quant-l1-p29]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Probability Foundations]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p29]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p29]
