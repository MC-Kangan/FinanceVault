## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Fiscal policy changes taxation and spending, while monetary policy changes money and credit to support stable growth and inflation.[^eco-l1-p27]

### Interpretation

Identify Monetary Policy within its market, cycle, policy, geopolitical, trade, or exchange-rate framework and distinguish its direction and decision use from adjacent concepts.[^eco-l1-p27]
