## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Equity Securities:** Common shares represent residual ownership and generally vote; dividends are discretionary. Callable common lets the issuer repurchase under specified terms; putable common gives holders a sale-back right.[^eq-l1-p38]
