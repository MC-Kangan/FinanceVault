# Side Letter

> [!summary]
> A side letter grants negotiated rights to a particular investor, such as most-favoured-nation or first-refusal provisions.[^alt-l1-p6]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A side letter grants negotiated rights to a particular investor, such as most-favoured-nation or first-refusal provisions.[^alt-l1-p6]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Foundations and Structures]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p6]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p6]
