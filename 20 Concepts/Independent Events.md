# Independent Events

> [!summary]
> Independent events are events for which the occurrence of one does not change the probability of the other.[^quant-l1-p24]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Independent events are events for which the occurrence of one does not change the probability of the other.[^quant-l1-p24]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Probability Foundations]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p24]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p24]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Probability Foundations]]. Compare it with [[Joint Probability]], [[Total Probability Rule]].
