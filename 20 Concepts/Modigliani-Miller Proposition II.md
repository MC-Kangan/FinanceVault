## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

MM Proposition I addresses firm value and Proposition II addresses required equity return, with taxes adding the debt tax shield.[^ci-l1-p37]

### Slide-level enrichment

Without taxes, MM Proposition I gives $V_L=V_U$ and Proposition II gives $r_e=r_0+(r_0-r_d)D/E$; the rising cost of equity offsets cheaper debt and WACC stays constant. With corporate taxes, $V_L=V_U+TD$ and $r_e=r_0+(r_0-r_d)(1-T)D/E$; the debt tax shield raises value and lowers WACC in the simplified model.[^ci-l1-p37]
