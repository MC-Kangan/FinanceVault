# Prediction Interval

> [!summary]
> A prediction interval gives a range for a future dependent-variable observation at a chosen confidence level.[^quant-l1-p58]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A prediction interval gives a range for a future dependent-variable observation at a chosen confidence level.[^quant-l1-p58]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Simple Linear Regression]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p58]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p58]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Simple Linear Regression]]. Compare it with [[Forecast Standard Error]], [[Indicator Variable]].
