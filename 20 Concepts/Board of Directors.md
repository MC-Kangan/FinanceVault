### Slide-level enrichment

Corporate governance is the system of internal controls and procedures by which a company is managed. Shareholder theory prioritizes shareholder-value maximization; stakeholder theory considers shareholders, directors, debtholders, customers, suppliers, employees, managers, and government while managing conflicts. Private debtholders may have more direct access to management and non-public information than public bondholders.[^ci-l1-p8]
