# Covariance

> [!summary]
> Covariance measures whether two variables tend to move together and in which direction.[^quant-l1-p18]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Covariance measures whether two variables tend to move together and in which direction.[^quant-l1-p18]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p18]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p18]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]]. Compare it with [[Scatter Plot]], [[Correlation]].
