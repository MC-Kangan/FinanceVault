> [!summary]
> Porter's Five Forces evaluates rivalry, entry threat, substitutes, supplier power, and customer power as drivers of industry profitability.[^fsa-l1-p85]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Porter's Five Forces evaluates rivalry, entry threat, substitutes, supplier power, and customer power as drivers of industry profitability.[^fsa-l1-p85]
