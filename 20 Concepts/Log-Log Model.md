# Log-Log Model

> [!summary]
> A log-log model relates logarithms of both variables, making the slope an elasticity.[^quant-l1-p59]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A log-log model relates logarithms of both variables, making the slope an elasticity.[^quant-l1-p59]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Simple Linear Regression]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p59]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p59]
