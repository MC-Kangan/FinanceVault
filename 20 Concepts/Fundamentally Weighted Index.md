## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Security Market Indexes:** Equal weighting gives equal weights and arithmetic-mean returns but creates small-cap bias and rebalancing needs. Fundamental weighting uses price-independent measures such as book value, cash flow, revenue, earnings, dividends, or employees, producing value/contrarian tilts.[^eq-l1-p25]

### Equity slide enrichment

An equal-weighted index assigns equal weights and averages constituent holding-period returns; it is simple but has small-cap bias and needs frequent rebalancing. Fundamental weighting uses price-independent size measures such as book value, cash flow, revenue, earnings, dividends, or employees, producing a value tilt and possible contrarian effect.[^eq-enrich-p25]
