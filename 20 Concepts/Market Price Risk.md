# Market Price Risk

> [!summary]
> Market price risk is the risk that a bond's price changes before sale because market yields change.[^fi-l1-p60]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Market price risk is the risk that a bond's price changes before sale because market yields change.[^fi-l1-p60]

### Exam focus

Identify the definition, direction of the principal relationships, and the financial-statement or valuation effect tested by this concept.[^fi-l1-p60]
