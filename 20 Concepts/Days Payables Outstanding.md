# Days Payables Outstanding

> [!summary]
> Days payables outstanding converts payables turnover into the average number of days taken to pay suppliers.[^fsa-l1-p44]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Days payables outstanding converts payables turnover into the average number of days taken to pay suppliers.[^fsa-l1-p44]

<!-- enrichment:fsa:start -->
### Formula

$$
Days\ payables\ outstanding=\frac{365}{Payables\ turnover}
$$[^fsa-detail-p44]
<!-- enrichment:fsa:end -->

<!-- enrichment:corporate-issuers:start -->

### Slide-level enrichment

The operating cycle is $DOH+DSO$; the cash conversion cycle is $DOH+DSO-DPO$ and measures the time from paying suppliers for inventory to collecting cash from sales. A shorter or negative cycle indicates more efficient operations in the source.[^ci-l1-p18]

For trade terms `a/m, net n`, the annualized cost of forgoing the discount is:[^ci-l1-p18]

$$
EAR=\left(1+\frac{a\%}{100\%-a\%}\right)^{\frac{365}{n-m}}-1
$$
[^ci-l1-p18]

Do not treat the discount percentage itself as the borrowing cost without adjusting the base and annualizing the period.[^ci-l1-p18]
