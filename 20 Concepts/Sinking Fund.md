# Sinking Fund

> [!summary]
> A sinking fund requires scheduled retirement of part of a bond issue, reducing credit risk while potentially creating reinvestment risk for investors.[^fi-l1-p8]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

A sinking fund requires scheduled retirement of part of a bond issue, reducing credit risk while potentially creating reinvestment risk for investors.[^fi-l1-p8]

### Exam focus

Identify the definition, direction of the principal relationships, and the financial-statement or valuation effect tested by this concept.[^fi-l1-p8]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

A bullet bond pays principal at maturity; a fully amortizing bond reduces principal to zero through scheduled payments; a partially amortizing bond leaves a balloon payment. A sinking fund retires principal over time, lowering credit risk but increasing reinvestment risk as principal is returned earlier.[^fi-features-p8]
