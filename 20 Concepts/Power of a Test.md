# Power of a Test

> [!summary]
> Test power is the probability of correctly rejecting a false null hypothesis.[^quant-l1-p43]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

The core relationship is $Power=1-P(\text{Type II error})=1-\beta$.[^quant-l1-p43]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Sampling and Inference]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p43]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p43]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Sampling and Inference]]. Compare it with [[Type II Error]], [[One-Sample Mean Test]].
