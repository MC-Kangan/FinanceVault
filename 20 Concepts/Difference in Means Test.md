# Difference in Means Test

> [!summary]
> A difference-in-means test assesses whether two population means differ.[^quant-l1-p44]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A difference-in-means test assesses whether two population means differ.[^quant-l1-p44]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Sampling and Inference]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p44]

### Common pitfalls
