# Descriptive Statistics

> [!summary]
> Descriptive statistics summarize and organize the observed features of a data set.[^quant-l1-p12]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Descriptive statistics summarize and organize the observed features of a data set.[^quant-l1-p12]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p12]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p12]
