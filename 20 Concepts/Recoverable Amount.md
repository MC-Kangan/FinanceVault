# Recoverable Amount

> [!summary]
> Under IFRS, recoverable amount is the higher of fair value less costs to sell and value in use.[^fsa-l1-p55]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Under IFRS, recoverable amount is the higher of fair value less costs to sell and value in use.[^fsa-l1-p55]

<!-- enrichment:fsa:start -->
### Impairment measurement

$$
Recoverable\ amount=\max(Fair\ value\ less\ costs\ to\ sell,Value\ in\ use)
$$[^fsa-detail-p55]

Value in use is the present value of expected future cash flows. The IFRS loss is carrying amount less recoverable amount; the source's US-GAAP sequence first tests recoverability with undiscounted cash flows and then measures loss using fair value.[^fsa-detail-p55]

An impairment reduces carrying value and net income but is noncash and therefore does not directly reduce CFO. Finite-lived intangibles follow the applicable framework; indefinite-lived intangibles are tested annually.[^fsa-detail-p56]
