## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Financial Markets and Trading:** Market orders prioritise immediacy; limit orders constrain price. A marketable limit order can execute immediately. All-or-none requires full execution; hidden and iceberg orders conceal all or part of size.[^eq-l1-p16]

### Equity slide enrichment

An all-or-none order executes only in full. A hidden order is visible only to the broker or exchange. An iceberg order exposes only a displayed portion of its total size.[^eq-enrich-p16]
