## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Working-capital policies trade liquidity and risk against return by varying current-asset holdings and reliance on short-term funding.[^ci-l1-p19]

### Interpretation

### Slide-level enrichment

The deck uses $Total\ working\ capital=Current\ assets-Current\ liabilities$ and defines net working capital after excluding cash, marketable securities, and short-term/current debt. A conservative policy holds more liquid operating assets and relies more on long-term funding; an aggressive policy minimizes excess current assets and relies more on short-term funding; a moderate policy lies between them.[^ci-l1-p19]
