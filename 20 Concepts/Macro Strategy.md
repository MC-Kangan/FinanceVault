# Macro Strategy

> [!summary]
> A macro strategy uses top-down economic views to trade expected movements in economic variables.[^alt-l1-p36]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A macro strategy uses top-down economic views to trade expected movements in economic variables.[^alt-l1-p36]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Hedge Funds]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p36]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p36]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Alternative Investments]] through [[Alternatives - Hedge Funds]]. Compare it with [[Opportunistic Strategy]], [[Managed Futures]].
