# GIPS Composite

> [!summary]
> A GIPS composite groups portfolios managed according to a similar strategy and prevents selective performance presentation.[^eth-l1-p96]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Avoid cherry-picking, retain terminated portfolios for the required historical period, and apply the deck's inclusion rules for discretionary and fee-paying portfolios.[^eth-l1-p96]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-l1-p96]
