## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Industry and Company Analysis:** Historical forecasting extrapolates past levels, trends, or relationships but can fail under structural change, cyclicality, or mean reversion.[^eq-l1-p62]

<!-- enrichment:equity:start -->

### Equity slide enrichment

Historical forecasts suit stable industries, low cycle sensitivity, immaterial items, and items without a distinct analyst view. For a cyclical company, an over-the-cycle average or median may suit a multi-year horizon.[^eq-enrich-p62]
