# Lessor Accounting

> [!summary]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

For a finance lease, the source derecognizes the leased asset and recognizes a lease receivable; receipts comprise interest and receivable recovery. For an operating lease, the lessor retains the asset and separately recognizes lease income and depreciation.[^fsa-lease-p65]

<!-- enrichment:fsa:start -->
### Finance versus operating lease

| Treatment | Initial accounting | Income pattern |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finance lease | Derecognize asset; recognize lease receivable | Declining interest income as the receivable amortizes |
| Operating lease | Keep underlying asset | Separate lease income and depreciation; level pattern in the source |
[^fsa-detail-p65]

Finance-lease interest equals opening lease receivable multiplied by the periodic rate, and the receivable follows the effective-interest method.[^fsa-detail-p65]
