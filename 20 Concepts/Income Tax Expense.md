# Income Tax Expense

> [!summary]
> Income tax expense reconciles current tax payable with changes in deferred tax balances.[^fsa-tax-p73]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Income tax expense reconciles current tax payable with changes in deferred tax balances.[^fsa-tax-p73][^fsa-tax-p78]

$$
\text{Income tax expense}=\text{Current tax payable}+\Delta DTL-\Delta DTA
$$
[^fsa-tax-p73]

$$
\text{Net income}=\text{Pretax accounting income}-\text{Income tax expense}
$$
[^fsa-tax-p73]

<!-- enrichment:fsa:start -->
### Tax reconciliation

$$
Income\ tax\ expense=Current\ tax\ payable+\Delta DTL-\Delta DTA
$$[^fsa-detail-p73]

Taxable income determines current tax payable, while pretax accounting income is the financial-reporting measure used in the net-income bridge.[^fsa-detail-p73]
<!-- enrichment:fsa:end -->
