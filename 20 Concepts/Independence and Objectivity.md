# Independence and Objectivity

> [!summary]
> Use reasonable care and judgment to preserve independence and objectivity, and reject benefits reasonably expected to compromise them.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p16][^eth-l1-p17][^eth-l1-p19]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Apply the standard to gifts, travel, issuer-paid research, internal pressure, compensation, and allocation decisions; distinguish client gifts from issuer inducements and disclose relevant arrangements.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p16][^eth-l1-p17][^eth-l1-p19]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p16][^eth-l1-p17][^eth-l1-p19]
