# Net Present Value

> [!summary]
> Net Present Value is a Level 1 concept in [[Corporate Issuers]]; its separate note preserves a reusable definition and a clear destination for later-level extensions.[^ci-l1-p25]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Capital budgeting uses after-tax incremental cash flows, includes opportunity costs and externalities, excludes sunk costs, and accepts positive-NPV independent projects.[^ci-l1-p25]

### Slide-level enrichment

Capital budgeting uses timed, after-tax incremental cash flows; includes positive or negative externalities and opportunity costs; and excludes sunk costs. Net present value is:[^ci-l1-p25]

$$
NPV=CF_0+\sum_{t=1}^{n}\frac{CF_t}{(1+r)^t}
$$
[^ci-l1-p25]

For an independent project, accept if $NPV>0$ and reject if $NPV<0$. NPV directly estimates the change in firm value but does not communicate project size.[^ci-l1-p25]
