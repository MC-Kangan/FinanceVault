# Corporate Credit Analysis

> [!summary]
> Corporate credit analysis combines qualitative business assessment with profitability, coverage, leverage, and cash-flow measures to judge an issuer's creditworthiness.[^fi-l1-p87]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Qualitative factors

Assess the business model, industry and competition, corporate governance, and business risk before interpreting the ratios. These factors shape the durability and volatility of the cash flows available for debt service.[^fi-l1-p87]

### Quantitative factors

- Profitability: EBIT margin, represented by its canonical concept [[Operating Profit Margin]].
- Coverage: [[Interest Coverage Ratio]] compares operating income with interest expense.
- Leverage: [[Debt-to-EBITDA Ratio]] compares debt with a cash-earnings proxy.
- Cash-flow capacity: retained cash flow to net debt compares retained cash generation with debt net of cash and marketable securities.[^fi-l1-p87]

$$
\text{EBIT Margin}=\frac{\text{Operating Income}}{\text{Revenue}}
$$
[^fi-l1-p87]

$$
\text{Interest Coverage}=\frac{\text{Operating Income}}{\text{Interest Expense}}
$$
[^fi-l1-p87]

$$
\text{Debt-to-EBITDA}=\frac{\text{Debt}}{\text{EBITDA}}
$$
[^fi-l1-p87]

$$
\text{RCF-to-Net-Debt}=\frac{\text{Retained Cash Flow}}{\text{Debt}-\text{Cash and Marketable Securities}}
$$
[^fi-l1-p87]

### Full-deck extension: Corporate Credit Analysis

Retain qualitative assessment of business model, industry and competition, corporate governance, and business risk, then linking quantitative profitability, coverage, leverage, and retained-cash-flow measures to the accounting concepts that generate them.[^fi-credit-p87]

$$
\text{EBIT margin}=\frac{\text{Operating income}}{\text{Revenue}}
$$
[^fi-credit-p87]

$$
\text{EBIT to interest expense}=\frac{\text{Operating income}}{\text{Interest expense}}
$$
[^fi-credit-p87]

$$
\text{Debt to EBITDA}=\frac{\text{Debt}}{\text{EBITDA}}
$$
[^fi-credit-p87]

$$
\text{RCF to net debt}=\frac{\text{Retained cash flow}}{\text{Debt}-\text{Cash and marketable securities}}
$$
[^fi-credit-p87]

Existing `Interest Coverage Ratio` and `Debt-to-EBITDA Ratio` remain the canonical destinations for the coverage and leverage formulas.[^fi-credit-p87]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

Corporate credit analysis combines business model, competition, governance, and business risk with profitability, coverage, leverage, and cash-flow measures. These accounting-derived measures are genuine bridges between Financial Statement Analysis and Fixed Income.[^fi-credit-p87]

$$EBIT\ margin=\frac{Operating\ income}{Revenue},\quad Interest\ coverage=\frac{Operating\ income}{Interest\ expense},\quad Debt/EBITDA=\frac{Debt}{EBITDA}$$
[^fi-credit-p87]
