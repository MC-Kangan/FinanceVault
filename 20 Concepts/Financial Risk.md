### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Industry and Company Analysis:** Financial leverage comes from fixed financing costs. Visually verified: $DFL=\%\Delta NI/\%\Delta EBIT=EBIT/(EBIT-I)$; $DTL=DFL\times DOL=\%\Delta NI/\%\Delta Q=(EBIT+F)/(EBIT-I)$.[^eq-l1-p53]

**Portfolio Management — Risk Management:** Risk identification covers financial risks—market, credit, liquidity—and non-financial risks such as operational, model, settlement, legal, regulatory, political, and reputational risk.[^pm-l1-p34]
