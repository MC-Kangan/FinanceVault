# Commodity Futures Pricing

> [!summary]
> Commodity futures pricing links spot price to financing, carry costs, convenience yield, and time.[^alt-l1-p31]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

$F_0(T)=S_0e^{(r+c-i)T}$, where $c$ is carry cost and $i$ is convenience yield.[^alt-l1-p31]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Natural Resources and Commodities]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p31]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p31]
