# Net Profit Margin

> [!summary]
> Net profit margin measures net income generated per unit of revenue and is a core profitability ratio.[^fsa-l1-p46]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula

$$
\text{Net Profit Margin}=\frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Revenue}}
$$
[^fsa-l1-p46]

### Interpretation

A higher net profit margin means more bottom-line profit is retained from each unit of revenue, after operating costs, financing effects, and taxes reflected in net income.[^fsa-l1-p46]

### Common pitfalls

Margin comparisons require consistent revenue and net-income definitions and attention to unusual items that may distort a period's profitability.[^fsa-l1-p46]

## Connections

- Net profit margin is the profitability component of three-part [[DuPont Analysis]].
- It helps explain [[Return on Equity]].
- Profitability is one quantitative input to [[Corporate Credit Analysis]].
