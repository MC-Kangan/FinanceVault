# Fundamental Long-Short Strategy

> [!summary]
> A fundamental long-short strategy buys attractive securities and shorts unattractive securities, sometimes using an index hedge.[^alt-l1-p34]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A fundamental long-short strategy buys attractive securities and shorts unattractive securities, sometimes using an index hedge.[^alt-l1-p34]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Hedge Funds]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p34]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p34]
