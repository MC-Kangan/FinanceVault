## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Capital-budgeting errors include forecast mistakes, inconsistent inflation treatment, ignored alternatives or competitors, and behavioral bias.[^ci-l1-p31]

### Interpretation

### Slide-level enrichment

Cognitive errors include internal forecasting mistakes, wrong overhead or discount rates, ignoring competitor responses, treating internal funds as free, and inconsistent inflation treatment; cash flows and discount rates must both be nominal or both real. Behavioral errors include inertia, reliance on accounting measures such as EPS or ROE, pet-project bias, and failure to consider alternatives.[^ci-l1-p31]
