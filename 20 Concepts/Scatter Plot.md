# Scatter Plot

> [!summary]
> A scatter plot displays paired observations to reveal direction, form, and strength of association.[^quant-l1-p17]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A scatter plot displays paired observations to reveal direction, form, and strength of association.[^quant-l1-p17]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p17]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p17]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]]. Compare it with [[Coefficient of Variation]], [[Covariance]].
