# Stratified Random Sampling

> [!summary]
> Stratified random sampling divides a population into strata and samples from each stratum.[^quant-l1-p35]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Stratified random sampling divides a population into strata and samples from each stratum.[^quant-l1-p35]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Sampling and Inference]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p35]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p35]
