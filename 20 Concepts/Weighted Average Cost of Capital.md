# Weighted Average Cost of Capital

> [!summary]
> Weighted Average Cost of Capital is a Level 1 concept in [[Corporate Issuers]]; its separate note preserves a reusable definition and a clear destination for later-level extensions.[^ci-l1-p34]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

WACC combines market-value financing weights with marginal component costs, including the tax-adjusted cost of debt.[^ci-l1-p34]

### Interpretation

Identify the role of Weighted Average Cost of Capital within its contract, decision rule, governance mechanism, financing choice, or operating framework, and distinguish it from adjacent concepts.[^ci-l1-p34]

$$

Use market-value weights when available, then target weights; otherwise infer targets from the current structure, trends or management statements, or comparable-company averages. Component costs are marginal, and only debt receives the tax adjustment: $After\text{-}tax\ cost\ of\ debt=YTM(1-T)$.[^ci-l1-p34]
