# CFA Institute Code of Ethics

> [!summary]
> The CFA Institute Code of Ethics sets six broad principles for integrity, client interests, independent judgment, professional conduct, market integrity, and continuing competence.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p7][^eth-l1-p8]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Members and candidates must uphold the six Code principles and the more detailed Standards of Professional Conduct.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p7][^eth-l1-p8]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p7][^eth-l1-p8]
