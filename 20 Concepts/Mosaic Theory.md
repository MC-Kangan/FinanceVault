# Mosaic Theory

> [!summary]
> Mosaic theory permits conclusions assembled from public information and nonmaterial nonpublic information.[^eth-l1-p33][^eth-l1-p34]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

The conclusion may itself be material; retain records supporting the research process and do not include material nonpublic information in the mosaic.[^eth-l1-p33][^eth-l1-p34]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-l1-p33][^eth-l1-p34]
