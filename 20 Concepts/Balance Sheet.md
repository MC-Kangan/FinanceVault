# Balance Sheet

> [!summary]
> A balance sheet reports a company's financial position by showing its resources, obligations, and residual owners' interest at a specific point in time.[^fsa-l1-p4]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition

The three core elements are assets controlled by the firm, liabilities owed to creditors, and owners' equity as the residual interest in net assets.[^fsa-l1-p4]

### Interpretation

Because it is a point-in-time statement, the balance sheet is a stock measure. Analysts combine it with period-based income and cash-flow information when calculating ratios and assessing financial flexibility.[^fsa-l1-p4]

> [!tip] 中文提示
> 资产负债表是某一时点的财务状况快照，不要把它与反映一段期间经营成果的利润表混淆。[^fsa-l1-p4]

### Common pitfalls

- Do not treat equity as a cash balance; it is the residual claim after liabilities.
- Remember that a balance-sheet amount is measured at a date, not over a period.[^fsa-l1-p4]
