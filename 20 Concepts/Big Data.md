# Big Data

> [!summary]
> Big data is characterized by high volume, velocity, variety, and concerns over veracity.[^quant-l1-p62]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Big data is characterized by high volume, velocity, variety, and concerns over veracity.[^quant-l1-p62]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Big Data Analysis]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p62]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p62]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Big Data Analysis]]. Compare it with [[Fintech]], [[Alternative Data]].
