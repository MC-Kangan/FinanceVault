# Secondary-Stage Infrastructure

> [!summary]
> Secondary-stage infrastructure consists of operational assets requiring little further development and usually has the lowest stage risk.[^alt-l1-p28]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Secondary-stage infrastructure consists of operational assets requiring little further development and usually has the lowest stage risk.[^alt-l1-p28]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Real Estate and Infrastructure]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p28]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p28]
