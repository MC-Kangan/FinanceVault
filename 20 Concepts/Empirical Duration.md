## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

- Analytical duration uses formulas and typically treats government yields and spreads as independent. Empirical duration uses historical statistical relationships and can reflect correlation between benchmark-rate and spread changes.[^fi-risk-p73]
- Under stress, government-bond empirical duration may remain close to analytical duration, while corporate-bond empirical duration can be lower because spread widening offsets benchmark-yield declines.[^fi-risk-p73]

### Exam focus

Identify the definition, direction of the principal relationships, and the financial-statement or valuation effect tested by this concept.[^fi-risk-p73]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

Analytical duration comes from valuation formulas and commonly treats benchmark-yield changes and spreads as independent. Empirical duration uses historical statistical relationships and can capture spread correlation and nonparallel moves; the deck notes it may be lower than analytical duration for corporate bonds.[^fi-risk-p73]
