# Preservation of Confidentiality

> [!summary]
> Protect information about current, former, and prospective clients except for specified legal, client-authorized, or misconduct-related circumstances.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p55][^eth-l1-p56]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Disclosure is allowed when required by law, permitted by the client, or concerning illegal client activity; cooperation with the Professional Conduct Program is also addressed.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p55][^eth-l1-p56]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p55][^eth-l1-p56]
