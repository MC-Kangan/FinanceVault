# Spurious Correlation

> [!summary]
> Spurious correlation is an observed association without a stable economic or causal basis.[^quant-l1-p19]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Spurious correlation is an observed association without a stable economic or causal basis.[^quant-l1-p19]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p19]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p19]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]]. Compare it with [[Correlation]], [[Skewness]].
