# Market Manipulation

> [!summary]
> Do not use information-based or transaction-based practices intended to distort prices or volume and mislead market participants.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p36][^eth-l1-p37]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Legitimate trading, tax-motivated transactions, liquidity provision, or compensation is not manipulation without deceptive intent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p36][^eth-l1-p37]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p36][^eth-l1-p37]
