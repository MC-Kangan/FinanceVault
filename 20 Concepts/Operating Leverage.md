## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Industry and Company Analysis:** Operating risk is operating-income sensitivity to units sold and rises with fixed operating costs. Visually verified: $DOL=\%\Delta EBIT/\%\Delta Q=Q(P-V)/[Q(P-V)-F]=(EBIT+F)/EBIT$.[^eq-l1-p52]

### Equity slide enrichment

Operating risk is EBIT sensitivity to units sold and rises with fixed operating cost. Fixed operating cost creates operating leverage:[^eq-enrich-p52]

$$
DOL=\frac{\%\Delta EBIT}{\%\Delta Q}=\frac{\Delta EBIT/EBIT}{\Delta Q/Q}=\frac{Q(P-V)}{Q(P-V)-F}=\frac{EBIT+F}{EBIT}
$$[^eq-enrich-p52]

### Slide-level enrichment

Economic and industry conditions affect debt and equity costs from the top down. Issuer-specific drivers include collateral and asset type, sales risk, profitability risk, operating leverage, financial leverage, and interest coverage. Stable, predictable, growing revenue lowers sales risk; a larger fixed-cost share raises operating leverage and profitability risk.[^ci-l1-p35]
