# Free Cash Flow

> [!summary]
> Free cash flow is cash available for distribution after meeting operating obligations and taxes without undermining required working-capital and fixed-capital investment.[^fsa-l1-p40]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Forms

- [[Free Cash Flow to the Firm]] measures cash available to all capital providers.
- [[Free Cash Flow to Equity]] measures cash available to common equity holders after debt-related flows.[^fsa-l1-p40]

### Interpretation

Free cash flow focuses on distributable economic cash generation rather than accounting profit alone. In credit analysis, retained cash flow relative to net debt is one quantitative indicator of an issuer's debt capacity.[^fsa-l1-p40][^fi-l1-p87]

> [!tip] 中文提示
> 自由现金流不是“账户里剩下的现金”，而是在维持经营和必要投资后，可供资本提供者分配的现金流。[^fsa-l1-p40]

### Common pitfalls

Always identify which claimholders the measure belongs to. FCFF and FCFE differ because interest after tax and net borrowing alter the cash attributable to debt and equity providers.[^fsa-l1-p40]

## Connections

- Use [[Free Cash Flow to the Firm]] for all-capital-provider cash flow.
- Use [[Free Cash Flow to Equity]] for common-equity cash flow.
- Apply cash-generation evidence directly in [[Corporate Credit Analysis]].
