### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Financial Markets and Trading:** Short selling borrows and sells securities, later repurchasing them for return to the lender. The short seller posts collateral, may receive a rebate net of fees, and owes payments in lieu of distributions.[^eq-l1-p13]
