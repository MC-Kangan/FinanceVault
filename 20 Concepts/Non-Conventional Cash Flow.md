# Non-Conventional Cash Flow

> [!summary]
> A project cash-flow pattern that changes sign more than once and can create multiple IRRs or no IRR. It is a distinct Level 1 link target within [[Corporate Issuers]].[^ci-l1-p26]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

A project cash-flow pattern that changes sign more than once and can create multiple IRRs or no IRR.[^ci-l1-p26]

### Slide-level enrichment

IRR is the discount rate satisfying $0=CF_0+\sum_{t=1}^{n}CF_t/(1+IRR)^t$. It communicates profitability, but non-conventional cash flows may produce multiple IRRs or no IRR and the method assumes reinvestment at the IRR. For conventional independent projects, NPV and IRR agree: accept when NPV is positive and IRR exceeds the cost of capital.[^ci-l1-p26]
