# Free Cash Flow to the Firm

> [!summary]
> Free cash flow to the firm (FCFF) is the cash available to both debt and equity capital providers after operating and reinvestment needs.[^fsa-l1-p40]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula from net income

$$
\text{FCFF}=\text{NI}+\text{Interest}(1-t)+\text{NCC}-\text{WCInv}-\text{FCInv}
$$
[^fsa-l1-p40]

Here, NI is net income, NCC is non-cash charges, WCInv is investment in working capital, and FCInv is investment in fixed capital.[^fsa-l1-p40]

### Formula from cash flow from operations

$$
\text{FCFF}=\text{CFO}+\text{Interest}(1-t)-\text{FCInv}
$$
[^fsa-l1-p40]

### Interpretation

After-tax interest is added back because FCFF is measured before distributing cash between debt and equity claimholders.[^fsa-l1-p40]

### Common pitfalls

Do not subtract net borrowing in FCFF: financing flows allocate cash among capital providers rather than determine the total cash available to them.[^fsa-l1-p40]

$$[^eq-enrich-p71][^eq-enrich-p74]
