### Formula and mechanics

**Portfolio Management — Modern Portfolio Theory and Asset Pricing:** Single-asset holding-period return combines income and price change. Expected return is probability-weighted return; variance/standard deviation measure dispersion. Investors may be risk-averse, risk-neutral, or risk-seeking.[^pm-l1-p4]
