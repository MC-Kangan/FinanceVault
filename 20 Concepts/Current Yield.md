# Current Yield

> [!summary]
> Current yield equals annual coupon income divided by the bond's current price and ignores capital gains, losses, and reinvestment.[^fi-l1-p46]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Current yield equals annual coupon income divided by the bond's current price and ignores capital gains, losses, and reinvestment.[^fi-l1-p46]

### Exam focus

Identify the definition, direction of the principal relationships, and the financial-statement or valuation effect tested by this concept.[^fi-l1-p46]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

Current yield equals annual coupon divided by flat price and omits time value, accrued interest, and capital gain or loss. Simple yield adds straight-line annual amortization of discount or premium before dividing by flat price.[^fi-valuation-p45][^fi-valuation-p46]
