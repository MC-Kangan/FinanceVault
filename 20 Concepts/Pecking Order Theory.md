### Slide-level enrichment

More debt can reduce agency costs of equity by committing free cash flow to debt service and limiting managerial misuse. Greater information asymmetry increases returns demanded by debt and equity suppliers. Pecking-order theory ranks financing as $Internal\ capital>Debt>External\ equity$ because external financing carries greater signaling and information costs.[^ci-l1-p39]
