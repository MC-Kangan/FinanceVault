# Multiple of Invested Capital

> [!summary]
> Multiple of invested capital compares realized plus unrealized value with total invested capital but does not capture how long realization takes.[^alt-l1-p8]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

$MOIC=\dfrac{Realized\ value+Unrealized\ value}{Total\ invested\ capital}$[^alt-l1-p8]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Foundations and Structures]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p8]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p8]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Alternative Investments]] through [[Alternatives - Foundations and Structures]]. Compare it with [[Money-Weighted Return]], [[Leveraged Return]].
