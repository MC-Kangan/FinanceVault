# Convertible Bond

> [!summary]
> Define a convertible bond as a debt-equity hybrid that gives the investor the right to exchange the bond for issuer shares. The conversion option raises value and generally permits a lower coupon or yield than straight debt.[^fi-features-p13]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

- Define a convertible bond as a debt-equity hybrid that gives the investor the right to exchange the bond for issuer shares. The conversion option raises value and generally permits a lower coupon or yield than straight debt.[^fi-features-p13]

$$
V_{\text{convertible}}=V_{\text{straight}}+V_{\text{call option on equity}}
$$
[^fi-features-p13]

$$
\text{Conversion price}=\frac{\text{Par value}}{\text{Conversion ratio}}
$$
[^fi-features-p13]

$$
\text{Conversion value}=\text{Share price}\times\text{Conversion ratio}
$$
[^fi-features-p13]

- Connect contingent convertibles, which convert when a trigger is reached, and warrants, which are detachable options rather than embedded conversion rights.[^fi-features-p14]

### Exam focus

Identify the definition, direction of the principal relationships, and the financial-statement or valuation effect tested by this concept.[^fi-features-p13]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

A convertible combines debt with an investor option to exchange the bond for a specified number of common shares; it commonly has a lower yield and higher price than straight debt.[^fi-features-p13]

$$Conversion\ price=\frac{Bond\ par\ value}{Conversion\ ratio},\qquad Conversion\ value=Stock\ price\times Conversion\ ratio$$
[^fi-features-p13]
