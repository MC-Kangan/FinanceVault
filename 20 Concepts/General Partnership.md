### Slide-level enrichment

The source compares organizational forms across legal identity, operation, liability, taxation, and financing. A corporation is an independent legal entity operated by managers and a board, provides limited liability, pays corporate tax on profits while shareholders pay personal tax on dividends, and has broad access to capital. Sole proprietorships and general partnerships are not independent entities, use pass-through personal taxation, have unlimited liability, and depend on owner or partner financing. A limited partnership is run by the general partner: the GP has unlimited liability and LPs have limited liability.[^ci-l1-p4]
