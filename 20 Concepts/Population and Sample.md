# Population and Sample

> [!summary]
> A population is the complete set of interest; a sample is a subset used to draw inferences.[^quant-l1-p12]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A population is the complete set of interest; a sample is a subset used to draw inferences.[^quant-l1-p12]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p12]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p12]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]]. Compare it with [[Descriptive Statistics]], [[Parameter and Sample Statistic]].
