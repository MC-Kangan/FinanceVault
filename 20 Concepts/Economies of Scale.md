# Economies of Scale

> [!summary]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Industry and Company Analysis:** Economies of scale reduce average cost as output grows; economies of scope reduce cost by producing related products together.[^eq-l1-p49]

### Economics applications

<!-- merged:economics-applications -->

**Economics application:** Cost curves distinguish fixed, variable, average, and marginal costs; long-run scale effects determine minimum efficient scale. Use [[Economics - Firms and Market Structures]] for chapter context.[^eco-l1-p6]

<!-- enrichment:equity:start -->

### Equity slide enrichment

Economies of scale reduce unit cost as output expands, usually by spreading fixed cost but sometimes through bargaining power that lowers variable cost. Economies of scope reduce unit cost as product or business lines expand by sharing costs across them.[^eq-enrich-p49]
