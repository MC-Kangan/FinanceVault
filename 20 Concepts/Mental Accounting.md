## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Market Efficiency and Behavioral Explanations:** Mental accounting separates fungible wealth into buckets; conservatism slows belief updating; narrow framing evaluates decisions outside total-portfolio context.[^eq-l1-p35]

**Portfolio Management — Behavioral Biases:** Cognitive errors include belief perseverance (conservatism, confirmation, representativeness, illusion of control, hindsight) and processing errors (anchoring/adjustment, mental accounting, framing, availability).[^pm-l1-p28]
