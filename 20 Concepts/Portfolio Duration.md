## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Aggregate-cash-flow duration is theoretically correct but difficult in practice. A weighted average of constituent durations is more practical and becomes more accurate when constituent yields are similar and the yield curve is flat. The weighted-average approach assumes a parallel yield-curve shift.[^fi-risk-p68]

### Exam focus

Identify the definition, direction of the principal relationships, and the financial-statement or valuation effect tested by this concept.[^fi-risk-p68]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

Greater convexity improves upside when yields fall and limits downside when yields rise, all else equal. Longer maturity, lower coupon, lower yield, and greater cash-flow dispersion generally increase convexity. Portfolio measures may be market-value weighted, subject to parallel-shift and flat-curve assumptions.[^fi-risk-p66][^fi-risk-p67][^fi-risk-p68]
