## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

- Residential MBS are backed by residential mortgage pools. Agency RMBS receive federal-agency or GSE support, while non-agency RMBS rely more heavily on credit enhancement.[^fi-structured-p99]
- A mortgage pass-through gives investors proportional participation in pool cash flows. Store weighted average coupon, weighted average maturity, weighted average life, pass-through rate, and the waterfall concept as core mechanics.[^fi-structured-p99]

### Exam focus

Identify the definition, direction of the principal relationships, and the financial-statement or valuation effect tested by this concept.[^fi-structured-p99]

### Alternative Investments context

Mortgage-backed securities provide public debt exposure to pools of mortgage cash flows.[^alt-l1-p24]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

Agency and non-agency RMBS differ in support and credit enhancement. Mortgage pass-through investors receive proportional pool cash flows; important measures include weighted average coupon, weighted average maturity, weighted average life, and pass-through rate.[^fi-structured-p99]
