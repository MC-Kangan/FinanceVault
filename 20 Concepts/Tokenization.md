# Tokenization

> [!summary]
> Tokenization represents ownership rights to an asset on a blockchain or distributed ledger.[^alt-l1-p42]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Tokenization represents ownership rights to an asset on a blockchain or distributed ledger.[^alt-l1-p42]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Digital Assets]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p42]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p42]
