# Loyalty Prudence and Care

> [!summary]
> Act for clients, exercise prudent judgment, and place client interests before employer or personal interests.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p39][^eth-l1-p41][^eth-l1-p43]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Identify the actual client or beneficiaries and apply fiduciary duties to brokerage, soft-dollar, and proxy-voting decisions.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p39][^eth-l1-p41][^eth-l1-p43]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p39][^eth-l1-p41][^eth-l1-p43]
