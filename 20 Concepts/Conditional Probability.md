# Conditional Probability

> [!summary]
> Conditional probability is the probability of an event given that another event has occurred.[^quant-l1-p24]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Conditional probability is the probability of an event given that another event has occurred.[^quant-l1-p24]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Probability Foundations]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p24]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p24]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Probability Foundations]]. Compare it with [[Expected Value]], [[Joint Probability]].
