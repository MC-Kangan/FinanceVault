# Distressed Debt

> [!summary]
> Distressed debt investing purchases obligations of financially troubled companies in anticipation of restructuring or recovery.[^alt-l1-p20]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Distressed debt investing purchases obligations of financially troubled companies in anticipation of restructuring or recovery.[^alt-l1-p20]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Private Capital]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p20]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p20]
