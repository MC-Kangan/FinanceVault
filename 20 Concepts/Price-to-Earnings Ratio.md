# Price-to-Earnings Ratio

> [!summary]
> Price-to-Earnings Ratio is a Level 1 link target within Equity Valuation; keeping it atomic makes its definition, mechanics, and later-level extensions directly reusable.[^eq-l1-p74]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Equity Valuation:** FCFE valuation: $V_0=\sum_{t=1}^{\infty}FCFE_t/(1+r)^t$. FCFE is dividend-paying capacity and can value non-dividend payers. Price multiples use earnings/book/sales/cash flow; trailing uses current/historical and leading uses forward denominators.[^eq-l1-p74]

**Equity Investments — Equity Valuation:** Visually verified justified P/E: $P_0/E_1=(1-b)/(r-g)$ and $P_0/E_0=(1-b)(1+g)/(r-g)$. P/E falls with required return and rises with growth; payout effects can be ambiguous. Multiples are intuitive but sensitive to accounting, cyclicality, and comparables.[^eq-l1-p75]

### Equity slide enrichment

Gordon-growth fundamentals imply:[^eq-enrich-p75]

$$
\frac{P_0}{E_1}=\frac{1-b}{r-g},\qquad \frac{P_0}{E_0}=\frac{(1-b)(1+g)}{r-g}
$$[^eq-enrich-p75]

P/E falls as required return rises and increases with growth; its relationship with payout is ambiguous. Multiples enable cross-sectional and time-series comparison but may conflict with DCF, lose accounting comparability, and become cycle-dependent.[^eq-enrich-p75]
