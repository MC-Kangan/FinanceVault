# Additional Compensation Arrangements

> [!summary]
> Obtain written consent from all affected parties before accepting compensation or benefits that compete with or may conflict with employer interests.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p63][^eth-l1-p64]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Consent must precede the arrangement and explain its terms sufficiently for parties to assess the conflict.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p63][^eth-l1-p64]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p63][^eth-l1-p64]
