## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Financial Markets and Trading:** Secondary markets may be call markets, with periodic batched clearing, or continuous markets, where trades occur whenever counterparties meet. Call markets concentrate liquidity; continuous markets favour immediacy.[^eq-l1-p9]

### Equity slide enrichment

A call market accumulates orders and selects a single price that maximizes executable volume; matching is easier but limited to specified times. A continuous market permits trading whenever open, but counterparties arriving at different times may miss each other.[^eq-enrich-p9]
