# Infrastructure Risk and Return

> [!summary]
> Infrastructure can offer stable long-term cash flows, inflation protection, barriers to entry, and diversification, with project and financing risks.[^alt-l1-p29]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Infrastructure can offer stable long-term cash flows, inflation protection, barriers to entry, and diversification, with project and financing risks.[^alt-l1-p29]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Real Estate and Infrastructure]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p29]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p29]
