## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Value-chain design explains how the firm creates value; profitability and unit economics connect operating margin, per-unit revenue and cost, and break-even volume.[^ci-l1-p43]

### Interpretation

Identify the role of Value Chain within its contract, decision rule, governance mechanism, financing choice, or operating framework, and distinguish it from adjacent concepts.[^ci-l1-p43]

### Common pitfalls

<!-- enrichment:corporate-issuers:start -->

### Slide-level enrichment

The value chain comprises systems and processes performed within a company to create customer value; the supply chain spans the broader flow across organizations. Profitability can be viewed through company-level operating margin, per-unit revenue and cost, and the break-even activity level.[^ci-l1-p43]
