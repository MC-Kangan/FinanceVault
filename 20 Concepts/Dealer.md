## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Financial Markets and Trading:** Dealers trade as principals, provide liquidity, and earn the bid–ask spread; broker-dealers combine agency and principal roles. Securitizers pool assets, arbitrageurs trade price discrepancies, deposit institutions channel savings, insurers pool risk, and settlement/custody institutions complete and safeguard trades.[^eq-l1-p6]

### Equity slide enrichment

A broker searches for counterparties without trading against the client; a block broker serves large orders. A dealer trades from inventory and supplies liquidity. Combining the roles can create an agency-versus-principal conflict. An investment bank advises issuers and arranges transactions.[^eq-enrich-p5]
