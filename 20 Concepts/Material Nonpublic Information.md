# Material Nonpublic Information

> [!summary]
> A person possessing material nonpublic information that could affect investment value must not trade or cause others to trade.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p30][^eth-l1-p31][^eth-l1-p33]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Assess materiality from likely market impact and source reliability, and treat information as public only after broad market dissemination.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p30][^eth-l1-p31][^eth-l1-p33]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p30][^eth-l1-p31][^eth-l1-p33]
