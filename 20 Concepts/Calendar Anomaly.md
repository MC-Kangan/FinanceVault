### Equity slide enrichment

Calendar effects in the deck include January or turn-of-the-year, turn-of-the-month, day-of-the-week, weekend, and holiday effects. The January effect is linked to tax-loss selling and window dressing; turn-of-the-month covers the last trading day and first three trading days of the next month.[^eq-enrich-p31][^eq-enrich-p32]
