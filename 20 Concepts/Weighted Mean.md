# Weighted Mean

> [!summary]
> A weighted mean assigns different importance to observations before averaging them.[^quant-l1-p13]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

The core relationship is $\bar{x}_w=\sum_{i=1}^{n}w_i x_i$, where $\sum_i w_i=1$.[^quant-l1-p13]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p13]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p13]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Descriptive Statistics]]. Compare it with [[Arithmetic Mean]], [[Trimmed Mean]].
