## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Equity Securities:** Preferred is generally less risky than common; putable, cumulative, and participating features alter risk/return. ROE is accounting return, cost of equity is required market return; book equity differs from market equity.[^eq-l1-p42]

<!-- enrichment:equity:start -->

### Equity slide enrichment

Book value of equity equals assets minus liabilities; market value equals price times shares outstanding. They are rarely equal: management directly affects book value but only indirectly affects market value. Cost of equity is used as a proxy for investors' minimum required return.[^eq-enrich-p42]
