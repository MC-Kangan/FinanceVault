# Static Trade-Off Theory

> [!summary]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Static trade-off theory balances the tax benefit of debt against the present value of direct and indirect financial-distress costs.[^ci-l1-p38]

### Slide-level enrichment

Direct distress costs are bankruptcy-related cash expenses such as legal and administrative fees; indirect costs include forgone investments, impaired ability to operate, and agency costs. Static trade-off theory expresses value as $V_L=V_U+TD-PV(Costs\ of\ financial\ distress)$: optimal leverage balances the marginal tax shield against expected distress costs.[^ci-l1-p38]
