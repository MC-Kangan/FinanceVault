# Expected Value

> [!summary]
> Expected value is the probability-weighted average of a random variable's possible outcomes.[^quant-l1-p23]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

The core relationship is $E(X)=\sum_i p_i x_i$.[^quant-l1-p23]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Probability Foundations]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p23]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p23]

## Connections

This concept belongs to [[Quantitative Methods]] through [[Quant - Probability Foundations]]. Compare it with [[Random Variable]], [[Conditional Probability]].
