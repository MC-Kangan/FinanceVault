### Slide-level enrichment

Accept all positive-NPV independent projects when funding is unlimited. Mutually exclusive projects compete directly, so select the highest positive NPV. Capital rationing imposes a fixed funding pool; sequencing requires projects in time order. NPV and IRR rankings may conflict because cash-flow size or timing differs and because NPV assumes reinvestment at the cost of capital while IRR assumes reinvestment at the IRR.[^ci-l1-p28]
