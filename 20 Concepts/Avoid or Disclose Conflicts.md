# Avoid or Disclose Conflicts

> [!summary]
> Avoid conflicts or disclose matters reasonably expected to impair independence or interfere with duties using prominent, plain-language communication.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p79][^eth-l1-p80]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Disclose beneficial ownership, board service, compensation arrangements, and other material conflicts effectively to clients, prospects, and employers.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p79][^eth-l1-p80]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p79][^eth-l1-p80]
