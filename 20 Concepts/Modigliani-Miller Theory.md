## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Modigliani-Miller analysis starts from idealized capital markets and separates financing decisions from investment decisions.[^ci-l1-p36]

### Slide-level enrichment

Capital structure responds to capital-market and economic conditions, industry and regulation, business model, asset ownership, life-cycle stage, cash flow, and profitability. Capital-intensive businesses tend toward more leverage than capital-light businesses. Start-ups have negative cash flow, high risk, and little debt availability; growth firms have rising revenue and cash flow; mature firms have slowing growth, stable cash flow, low risk, and high debt capacity.[^ci-l1-p36]

MM assumes homogeneous expected cash flows, risk-free investor borrowing/lending, perfect markets with no transaction, bankruptcy, or agency costs and symmetric information, plus independence of financing and investment decisions.[^ci-l1-p36]
