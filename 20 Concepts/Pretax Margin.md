# Pretax Margin

> [!summary]
> Pretax margin equals earnings before tax divided by revenue and reflects operating and financing effects before tax.[^fsa-l1-p46]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Pretax margin equals earnings before tax divided by revenue and reflects operating and financing effects before tax.[^fsa-l1-p46]

<!-- enrichment:fsa:start -->
### Formula

$$
Pretax\ margin=\frac{EBT}{Revenue}
$$[^fsa-detail-p46]
