# Pre-Seed Capital

> [!summary]
> Pre-seed or angel capital funds a business at the idea stage.[^alt-l1-p17]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Pre-seed or angel capital funds a business at the idea stage.[^alt-l1-p17]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Private Capital]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p17]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p17]
