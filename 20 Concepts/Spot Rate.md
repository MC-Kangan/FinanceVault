# Spot Rate

> [!summary]
> Define a spot or zero rate as the yield on a zero-coupon bond maturing at the date of the relevant cash flow, and use a different spot rate to discount each cash flow.[^fi-valuation-p52]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

- Define a spot or zero rate as the yield on a zero-coupon bond maturing at the date of the relevant cash flow, and use a different spot rate to discount each cash flow.[^fi-valuation-p52]

$$
V_0=\sum_{t=1}^{N}\frac{CF_t}{(1+s_t)^t}
$$
[^fi-valuation-p52]

### Exam focus

Identify the definition, direction of the principal relationships, and the financial-statement or valuation effect tested by this concept.[^fi-valuation-p52]

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

Spot rates discount individual cash flows; the par rate makes a bond price equal par; forward rates are future-period rates implied by current spot rates. Spot, par, and forward curves can be upward sloping, flat, or inverted.[^fi-valuation-p52][^fi-valuation-p53][^fi-valuation-p54]

$$\left(1+s_A\right)^A\left(1+IFR_{A,B-A}\right)^{B-A}=\left(1+s_B\right)^B$$
[^fi-valuation-p53]
