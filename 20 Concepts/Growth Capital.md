# Growth Capital

> [!summary]
> Growth capital takes minority or controlling stakes in more mature companies seeking expansion.[^alt-l1-p18]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

Growth capital takes minority or controlling stakes in more mature companies seeking expansion.[^alt-l1-p18]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Private Capital]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p18]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p18]
