# Operating Profit Margin

> [!summary]
> Operating profit margin equals operating income divided by revenue and measures profitability from core operations before financing and tax.[^fsa-l1-p46]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Operating profit margin equals operating income divided by revenue and measures profitability from core operations before financing and tax.[^fsa-l1-p46]

<!-- enrichment:fsa:start -->
### Formula

$$
Operating\ profit\ margin=\frac{Operating\ profit}{Revenue}
$$[^fsa-detail-p46]
<!-- enrichment:fsa:end -->

### Slide-deck enrichment

<!-- merged:fi-enrichment -->

Corporate credit analysis combines business model, competition, governance, and business risk with profitability, coverage, leverage, and cash-flow measures. These accounting-derived measures are genuine bridges between Financial Statement Analysis and Fixed Income.[^fi-credit-p87]

$$EBIT\ margin=\frac{Operating\ income}{Revenue},\quad Interest\ coverage=\frac{Operating\ income}{Interest\ expense},\quad Debt/EBITDA=\frac{Debt}{EBITDA}$$
[^fi-credit-p87]

<!-- enrichment:corporate-issuers:start -->

### Slide-level enrichment

The value chain comprises systems and processes performed within a company to create customer value; the supply chain spans the broader flow across organizations. Profitability can be viewed through company-level operating margin, per-unit revenue and cost, and the break-even activity level.[^ci-l1-p43]
