# Public-Private Partnership

> [!summary]
> A public-private partnership contracts with the private sector to deliver a project or service traditionally provided publicly.[^alt-l1-p26]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

A public-private partnership contracts with the private sector to deliver a project or service traditionally provided publicly.[^alt-l1-p26]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Alternatives - Real Estate and Infrastructure]] and identify its position in the relevant structure, lifecycle, strategy, or risk-return comparison.[^alt-l1-p26]

### Common pitfalls

Distinguish ownership from financing, direct from indirect exposure, and a strategy's stated source of return from its implementation risks.[^alt-l1-p26]
