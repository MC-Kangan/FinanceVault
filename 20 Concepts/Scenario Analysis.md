# Scenario Analysis

> [!summary]
> Scenario Analysis is a Level 1 link target within Industry and Company Analysis and Risk Management; keeping it atomic makes its definition, mechanics, and later-level extensions directly reusable.[^eq-l1-p67]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Industry and Company Analysis:** Leverage forecasts connect debt, interest, covenants, and cash capacity. Scenario/sensitivity analysis tests statement, valuation, and solvency effects.[^eq-l1-p67]

**Portfolio Management — Risk Management:** Risk measurement can use probability, volatility, sensitivity, scenario/stress tests, value at risk, and conditional VaR/expected shortfall. Credit-risk measurement considers exposure, default likelihood, and loss severity.[^pm-l1-p35]

### Equity slide enrichment

Separate maintenance capex, often anchored to depreciation and adjusted for inflation, from discretionary growth capex tied to expansion and revenue growth. Project capital structure with leverage ratios, historical practice, financial strategy, and capex needs. Complete the forecast with alternative outcomes based on key risks and their likelihood.[^eq-enrich-p66][^eq-enrich-p67]
