## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Equity Investments — Industry and Company Analysis:** A business model explains value creation/capture. Revenue may be forecast bottom-up from operational units or top-down from market size, share, and growth.[^eq-l1-p46]

### Equity slide enrichment

Business-model analysis is the first step because it identifies drivers of financial results and position. Bottom-up revenue analysis decomposes volume and price or product, segment, and geography; top-down analysis uses market share, addressable market or market size, and GDP growth. Issuer, public third-party, proprietary third-party, and proprietary primary-research sources support the work.[^eq-enrich-p46]

<!-- enrichment:equity:end -->

<!-- enrichment:corporate-issuers:start -->

### Slide-level enrichment

A business model answers who the customers are, what is offered, where and through which channels it reaches them, and how much/how customers pay. Channels include traditional distribution, direct sales versus agency, and omnichannel. Pricing structures include value- or cost-based pricing, tiered and dynamic pricing, auctions, bundling, add-ons, razor-and-blades, penetration, freemium, hidden revenue, subscriptions, leasing, licensing, franchising, and fractionalization.[^ci-l1-p42]
