### Slide-level enrichment

AGMs cover recurring matters such as board elections and bylaw changes; EGMs handle exceptional matters such as acquisitions, takeovers, asset sales, capital increases, or liquidation. Proxy voting authorizes another party to vote. Shareholder mechanisms include activism and litigation. Takeover routes include proxy contests, tender offers, and hostile takeovers; staggered boards and poison pills are anti-takeover defenses.[^ci-l1-p10]
