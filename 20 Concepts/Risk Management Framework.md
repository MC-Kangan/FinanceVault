## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Portfolio Management — Risk Management:** A risk-management framework establishes governance, infrastructure, policies/processes, risk appetite/tolerance, identification, measurement, modification, and monitoring. Enterprise risk management considers interactions among risks rather than isolated silos.[^pm-l1-p33]
