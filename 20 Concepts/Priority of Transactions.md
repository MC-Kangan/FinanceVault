# Priority of Transactions

> [!summary]
> Client and employer transactions must take priority over transactions in which the member or candidate is beneficial owner.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p82][^eth-l1-p83]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Apply priority to relevant personal and family accounts; this standard governs sequencing, while Fair Dealing governs treatment among clients.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p82][^eth-l1-p83]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p82][^eth-l1-p83]
