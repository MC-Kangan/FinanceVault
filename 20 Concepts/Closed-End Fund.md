### Formula and mechanics

**Portfolio Management — Portfolio Management Process and IPS:** Open-end mutual funds issue/redeem at NAV; closed-end funds trade on exchanges and may deviate from NAV. ETFs trade intraday and use creation/redemption mechanisms that help keep price near NAV.[^pm-l1-p20]
