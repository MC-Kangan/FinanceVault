# Firm Supply Curve

> [!summary]
> Firm Supply Curve is a Level 1 Economics concept within [[Economics - Firms and Market Structures]] and a reusable destination for formulas, comparisons, and later-level extensions.[^eco-l1-p10]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

A firm shuts down in the short run when price cannot cover average variable cost and exits in the long run when price cannot cover average total cost.[^eco-l1-p10]

### Interpretation

Identify Firm Supply Curve within its market, cycle, policy, geopolitical, trade, or exchange-rate framework and distinguish its direction and decision use from adjacent concepts.[^eco-l1-p10]
