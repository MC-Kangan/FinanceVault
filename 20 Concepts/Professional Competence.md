# Professional Competence

> [!summary]
> Members and candidates must act with and maintain the competence necessary to fulfill their professional responsibilities.[^eth-code-p1]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

This current official standard is included from the 2024 CFA Institute source even though the older review deck does not develop it separately.[^eth-code-p1]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p1]
