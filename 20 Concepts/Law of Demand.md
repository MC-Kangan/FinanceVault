# Law of Demand

> [!summary]
> Law of Demand is a Level 1 Economics concept within [[Economics - Firms and Market Structures]] and a reusable destination for formulas, comparisons, and later-level extensions.[^eco-l1-p4]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Definition and mechanics

Demand slopes downward under the law of demand; economic profit subtracts both explicit and implicit opportunity costs, while normal profit makes economic profit zero.[^eco-l1-p4]

### Interpretation
