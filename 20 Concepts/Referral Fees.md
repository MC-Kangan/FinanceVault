# Referral Fees

> [!summary]
> Disclose compensation or benefits paid or received for recommending products or services to employers, clients, and prospects as appropriate.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p85][^eth-l1-p86]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Make disclosure before the relevant decision so recipients can assess the incentive and conflict.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p85][^eth-l1-p86]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p85][^eth-l1-p86]
