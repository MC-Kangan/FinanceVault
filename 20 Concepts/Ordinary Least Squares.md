# Ordinary Least Squares

> [!summary]
> Ordinary least squares chooses coefficients that minimize the sum of squared residuals.[^quant-l1-p53]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

The core relationship is $SSE=\sum_i(Y_i-\hat{Y}_i)^2$.[^quant-l1-p53]

### Interpretation

Use this concept within [[Quant - Simple Linear Regression]] and distinguish its role from neighbouring measures or methods before applying a formula.[^quant-l1-p53]

### Common pitfalls

Match the definition, inputs, direction, and units to the question; do not substitute a similarly named statistic or return measure.[^quant-l1-p53]
