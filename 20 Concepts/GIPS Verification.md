# GIPS Verification

> [!summary]
> GIPS verification is independent third-party assessment of firm-wide composite construction and related processes.[^eth-l1-p97]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Verification applies to the firm as a whole and does not guarantee the accuracy of any single performance presentation.[^eth-l1-p97]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-l1-p97]
