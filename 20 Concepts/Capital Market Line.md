# Capital Market Line

> [!summary]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Formula and mechanics

**Portfolio Management — Modern Portfolio Theory and Asset Pricing:** The optimal CAL is tangent to the risky-asset frontier at optimal risky portfolio Y; investor P combines Y with the risk-free asset according to risk preference. Under homogeneous expectations, the common optimal risky portfolio is the market portfolio. Visually verified CML: $E(R_p)=r_f+[(R_M-r_f)/\sigma_M]\sigma_p$.[^pm-l1-p9]

**Portfolio Management — Modern Portfolio Theory and Asset Pricing:** The SML plots expected return against beta; intercept is $r_f$ and slope is the market risk premium. Properly priced assets lie on it, overpriced below, underpriced above. The CML applies to efficient portfolios and total risk; the SML applies to all properly priced assets/portfolios and systematic risk.[^pm-l1-p13]
