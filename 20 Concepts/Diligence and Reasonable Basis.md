# Diligence and Reasonable Basis

> [!summary]
> Exercise diligence, independence, and thoroughness, and support analysis and recommendations with appropriate research.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p69][^eth-l1-p70][^eth-l1-p71]

## Level 1 - Foundation

### Requirement and exam focus

Reasonable reliance on others requires evaluating their process. Review quantitative models, group research, and outside advisers to establish an adequate basis.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p69][^eth-l1-p70][^eth-l1-p71]

### Common pitfall

Apply the requirement to the facts and intent of the scenario; do not decide solely from whether an action feels unfair or whether local law is silent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p69][^eth-l1-p70][^eth-l1-p71]
