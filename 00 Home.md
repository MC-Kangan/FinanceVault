## Optional study plugins

## Working rules

- English titles and links are canonical; Chinese explanations and aliases are supporting aids.
- Level tags show where trusted material exists; Level sections preserve what was taught at each level.
- Every managed claim cluster opens back to an exact PDF page.
- Add personal commentary only inside protected Personal Notes blocks or under `30 Personal/`.
