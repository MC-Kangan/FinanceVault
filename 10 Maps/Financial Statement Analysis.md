# Financial Statement Analysis

## Statement foundations

- [[Balance Sheet]] describes financial position at a point in time.
- [[Accounting Equation]] connects assets, liabilities, and owners' equity.

## Ratio analysis

- Liquidity: [[Current Ratio]]
- Solvency and credit: [[Interest Coverage Ratio]] and [[Debt-to-EBITDA Ratio]]
