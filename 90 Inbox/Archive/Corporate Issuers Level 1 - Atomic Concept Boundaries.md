# Corporate Issuers Level 1 - Atomic Concept Boundaries

## Rationale

The deck is already a condensed review source. Named organizational forms, governance mechanisms, liquidity measures, capital-budgeting rules, capital-structure theories, and business-model patterns therefore become canonical notes when they own distinct exam behavior, a formula, or a useful link destination. Existing nodes are enriched rather than duplicated.

## Proposed concepts by chapter

### Organization and Governance

Create or enrich: `Sole Proprietorship`, `General Partnership`, [[Limited Partnership]], `Corporation`, `Public Company`, `Private Company`, [[Initial Public Offering]], [[Direct Listing]], `Take-Private Transaction`, `Debt Financing`, `Equity Financing`, `Corporate Governance`, `Shareholder Theory`, `Stakeholder Theory`, `Principal-Agent Problem`, `Managerial Entrenchment`, `Controlling Shareholder`, `Dual-Class Share Structure`, `Board of Directors`, `Annual General Meeting`, `Extraordinary General Meeting`, `Proxy Voting`, `Shareholder Activism`, `Proxy Contest`, `Tender Offer`, `Hostile Takeover`, `Staggered Board`, `Poison Pill`, `Audit Committee`, `Remuneration Committee`, `Risk Committee`, `Physical Climate Risk`, and `Transition Climate Risk`.[^ci-boundaries-p4][^ci-boundaries-p13]

Governance conflicts and mechanisms remain separate because the deck distinguishes the parties, incentives, voting tools, committees, creditor protections, and takeover defenses. General Partner and Limited Partner continue to resolve to their existing Alternative Investments nodes rather than being recreated.

### Working Capital and Liquidity

Create or enrich: `Primary Liquidity Source`, `Secondary Liquidity Source`, [[Free Cash Flow]], `Liquidity Drag`, `Liquidity Pull`, [[Current Ratio]], [[Quick Ratio]], [[Cash Ratio]], [[Cash Conversion Cycle]], [[Days Inventory on Hand]], [[Days Sales Outstanding]], [[Days Payables Outstanding]], `Trade Credit`, `Effective Annual Rate`, `Total Working Capital`, `Net Working Capital`, `Conservative Working Capital Policy`, `Aggressive Working Capital Policy`, `Moderate Working Capital Policy`, and `Short-Term Funding`.[^ci-boundaries-p16][^ci-boundaries-p21]

The operating-cycle measures must link back to Financial Statement Analysis and Equity Investments. `Short-Term Financing` is already an alias of the existing `Short-Term Funding` node and must not become a duplicate.

### Capital Investments

Create or enrich: `Capital Investment`, `Maintenance Project`, `Compliance Project`, `Expansion Project`, `After-Tax Incremental Cash Flow`, `Externality`, `Opportunity Cost`, `Sunk Cost`, `Net Present Value`, [[Money-Weighted Return]], [[Return on Invested Capital]], `Independent Project`, `Mutually Exclusive Project`, `Capital Rationing`, `Project Sequencing`, `NPV Profile`, `Crossover Rate`, `Real Option`, `Timing Option`, `Abandonment Option`, `Growth Option`, `Flexibility Option`, `Fundamental Option`, and `Capital Budgeting Error`.[^ci-boundaries-p24][^ci-boundaries-p31]

`Internal Rate of Return` and `IRR` remain aliases of the existing [[Money-Weighted Return]] note. NPV, ROIC, and the named real options remain atomic because they have distinct formulas or decision rules.

### Financing and Capital Structure

Create or enrich: `Weighted Average Cost of Capital`, `Target Capital Structure`, `Cost of Debt`, `Cost of Preferred Stock`, `Cost of Equity`, `Operating Leverage`, `Financial Leverage`, `Modigliani-Miller Theory`, `Modigliani-Miller Proposition I`, `Modigliani-Miller Proposition II`, `Cost of Financial Distress`, `Static Trade-Off Theory`, `Asymmetric Information Cost`, `Agency Cost of Equity`, `Free Cash Flow Hypothesis`, and `Pecking Order Theory`.[^ci-boundaries-p34][^ci-boundaries-p39]

`Weighted Average Cost of Capital` must be a distinct node from the inventory-accounting concept [[Weighted Average Cost]]. Tax and no-tax cases belong as clearly separated sections inside each Modigliani-Miller proposition rather than four near-duplicate files.

### Business Models

Create or enrich: `Business Model`, `Value Proposition`, `Distribution Channel`, `Pricing Power`, `Price Discrimination`, `Dynamic Pricing`, `Bundling`, `Razor-and-Blades Model`, `Penetration Pricing`, `Freemium Model`, `Subscription Model`, `Value Chain`, `Supply Chain`, [[Operating Profit Margin]], `Unit Economics`, `Break-Even Point`, `Private Label`, `Licensing`, `Franchise`, `Value-Added Reseller`, `Platform Business Model`, `Network Effect`, and `Crowdsourcing`.[^ci-boundaries-p42][^ci-boundaries-p44]

## Alias and cross-topic policy

- `WACC` resolves only to `Weighted Average Cost of Capital`; `IRR` and `Internal Rate of Return` continue to resolve to [[Money-Weighted Return]].
- `AGM`, `EGM`, `NPV`, `ROIC`, `MM`, and relevant Chinese translations become aliases only after normalized collision validation.
- Existing accounting ratios and operating-cycle measures receive Corporate Issuers context and source citations rather than duplicate files.
- Cross-topic links should connect governance to equity analysis, working capital to [[Financial Statement Analysis]], project appraisal to [[Quantitative Methods]], capital structure to [[Fixed Income]] and [[Equity Investments]], and operating margin to business-model economics only when the source-supported explanation is meaningful.
