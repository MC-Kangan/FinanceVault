# Ethics - Duties to Clients

## Exact proposed content

Create `Loyalty Prudence and Care`: act for clients, place their interests before employer or personal interests, identify the actual client or beneficiaries, exercise prudent judgment, and consider soft-dollar and proxy-voting responsibilities.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p39][^eth-l1-p41][^eth-l1-p43]

Create `Fair Dealing`: deal fairly and objectively when disseminating analysis, recommendations, actions, or professional services. Fair does not require identical service; differentiated service may be acceptable when disclosed and not disadvantaging other clients. Allocate oversubscribed issues fairly, commonly pro rata rather than by account size or favored status.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p45][^eth-l1-p46][^eth-l1-p47]

Create `Investment Suitability`: investigate and regularly update client experience, objectives, risk tolerance, and constraints; judge investments in total-portfolio context. For mandate-based portfolios, act consistently with stated objectives and constraints. Document the IPS and distinguish solicited from unsolicited trades.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p49][^eth-l1-p50]

Create `Performance Presentation`: make reasonable efforts to present performance fairly, accurately, and completely. Do not imply that one representative account is the whole firm's result, and provide supporting information when requested.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p52][^eth-l1-p53]

Create `Preservation of Confidentiality`: protect current, former, and prospective client information unless it concerns illegal activity, law requires disclosure, or the client permits disclosure. Cooperation with the Professional Conduct Program is also addressed by the deck.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p55][^eth-l1-p56]
