# Ethics - Introduction and Professionalism

## Rationale

Create the Ethics topic foundation and the five current Professionalism standards. The official 2024 CFA Institute sheet is authoritative for standard names; the review deck supplies exam-focused applications.

## Exact proposed content

Create `Ethics and Trust`, `CFA Institute Code of Ethics`, and `Professional Conduct Program`. Ethics concerns principles of conduct beyond minimum legal compliance; the Code expresses broad principles, while the Standards set enforceable professional requirements. The Professional Conduct Program investigates possible violations and may lead to cautionary letters or disciplinary sanctions, subject to the CFA Institute process.[^eth-l1-p5][^eth-l1-p6][^eth-code-p1]

Create `Knowledge of the Law`: understand applicable requirements, follow the strictest applicable rule when requirements conflict, do not knowingly participate in violations, and dissociate from misconduct. The deck emphasizes escalation, consultation, and complete dissociation when a violation continues.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p12][^eth-l1-p14]

Create `Independence and Objectivity`: use reasonable care and judgment to preserve independence; do not offer, solicit, or accept benefits reasonably expected to compromise objectivity. Cover issuer-paid research, gifts from clients versus issuers, travel, internal pressure, and written-consent interactions.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p16][^eth-l1-p17][^eth-l1-p19]

Create `Misrepresentation`: do not knowingly misstate analysis, recommendations, actions, qualifications, services, guarantees, models, benchmarks, or performance. Attribute others' work, including after changing employers, and exercise care when relying on third-party research.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p21][^eth-l1-p23][^eth-l1-p25]

Create `Misconduct`: prohibit dishonesty, fraud, deceit, or conduct that reflects adversely on professional reputation, integrity, or competence; distinguish purely personal behavior from conduct with a professional nexus.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p27][^eth-l1-p28]

Create `Professional Competence`: act with and maintain the competence necessary to fulfill professional responsibilities. This is present in the official 2024 source even though the older review deck does not develop it separately.[^eth-code-p1]
