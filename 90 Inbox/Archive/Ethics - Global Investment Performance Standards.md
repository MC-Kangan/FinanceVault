# Ethics - Global Investment Performance Standards

## Exact proposed content

Create `Global Investment Performance Standards`: GIPS are voluntary ethical standards for investment-performance presentation. Compliance is claimed firm-wide, not for a single fund or product, and firms must follow applicable law while disclosing conflicts between law and GIPS where required by the deck.[^eth-l1-p95]

Create `GIPS Composite`: a composite aggregates portfolios managed under a similar strategy. Avoid cherry-picking; include terminated portfolios through the appropriate historical period. Fee-paying discretionary portfolios must be included in at least one composite, while non-fee-paying discretionary and non-discretionary portfolios follow the deck's stated inclusion rules.[^eth-l1-p96]

Create `GIPS Verification`: verification is performed by an independent third party on a firm-wide basis and assesses composite construction and firm processes; it is not a guarantee of any specific presentation. Link the nine sections shown in the deck as the framework for further study.[^eth-l1-p97]
