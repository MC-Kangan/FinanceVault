# Ethics - Conflicts of Interest

## Exact proposed content

Create `Avoid or Disclose Conflicts` with alias `Disclosure of Conflicts`. Use the current official title: avoid conflicts or make full and fair disclosure of matters reasonably expected to impair independence and objectivity or interfere with duties; disclosure must be prominent, plain-language, and effective. Cover beneficial ownership, board service, and compensation arrangements.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p79][^eth-l1-p80]

Create `Priority of Transactions`: client and employer transactions take priority over transactions in which the member or candidate is beneficial owner. Treat personal and relevant family accounts according to beneficial ownership; the standard is about sequencing, while Fair Dealing governs treatment among clients.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p82][^eth-l1-p83]

Create `Referral Fees`: disclose to employers, clients, and prospects any compensation or benefit paid or received for recommending products or services, early enough for them to assess the conflict.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p85][^eth-l1-p86]
