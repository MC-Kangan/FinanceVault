# Ethics - Investment Analysis Recommendations and Actions

## Exact proposed content

Create `Diligence and Reasonable Basis`: exercise diligence, independence, and thoroughness; support analysis and recommendations with appropriate research. Reasonable reliance on others is possible after assessing their process. Cover quantitative-model review, group research, and selection of outside advisers.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p69][^eth-l1-p70][^eth-l1-p71]

Create `Communication with Clients and Prospective Clients`: disclose services and related client costs; explain the basic format and principles of investment processes, material changes, significant limitations and risks, and important factors; distinguish fact from opinion.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p73][^eth-l1-p74]

Create `Record Retention`: develop and maintain records supporting analyses, recommendations, actions, and investment-related communications. Records created for the employer generally belong to the employer; preserve relevant electronic and social-media records under applicable requirements.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p76][^eth-l1-p77]
