# Ethics - Integrity of Capital Markets

## Exact proposed content

Create `Material Nonpublic Information`: a person possessing material nonpublic information that could affect investment value must not trade or cause others to trade. Materiality depends on likely market impact and source reliability; public dissemination must reach the market broadly. Create `Mosaic Theory` for conclusions formed from public information plus nonmaterial nonpublic information, with supporting records retained.[^eth-code-p1][^eth-l1-p30][^eth-l1-p31][^eth-l1-p33]

Create `Information Barrier`: firms should restrict flows of material nonpublic information through appropriate controls; proprietary trading is particularly restricted when the firm possesses such information, while market-making and customer facilitation require careful policies.[^eth-l1-p34]

Create `Market Manipulation`: prohibit information-based or transaction-based practices intended to distort prices or artificially inflate volume and mislead participants. Legitimate trading, tax-motivated transactions, liquidity provision, and compensation arrangements are not violations without manipulative intent.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p36][^eth-l1-p37]
