# Ethics - Duties to Employers

## Exact proposed content

Create `Loyalty to Employer`: in employment matters act for the employer, do not deprive it of skills, divulge confidential information, or cause harm. Employees may prepare to leave but may not compete before departure; distinguish employer records from general knowledge and skill. Whistleblowing may be consistent with the standard when motivated by protecting clients or market integrity rather than personal gain.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p58][^eth-l1-p59][^eth-l1-p61]

Create `Additional Compensation Arrangements`: do not accept competing compensation or benefits that could create a conflict without written consent from all parties involved; obtain consent before the arrangement begins.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p63][^eth-l1-p64]

Create `Responsibilities of Supervisors`: make reasonable efforts to ensure supervised persons comply with law and the Code and Standards. Establish adequate systems, investigate indications of violations, and take corrective action rather than relying only on instructions or disclaimers.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p66][^eth-l1-p67]
