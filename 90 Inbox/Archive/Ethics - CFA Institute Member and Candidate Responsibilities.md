# Ethics - CFA Institute Member and Candidate Responsibilities

## Exact proposed content

Create `Conduct as Participants in CFA Institute Programs`: do not compromise the reputation, integrity, validity, or security of CFA Institute programs. Protect confidential exam information and follow program rules, while recognizing that discussion of curriculum facts and general exam experience may be permissible.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p88][^eth-l1-p89]

Create `Reference to CFA Institute the CFA Designation and the CFA Program`: do not exaggerate the meaning or implications of membership, the designation, or candidacy. Use CFA as an adjective, not a noun; do not imply guaranteed performance or compare the designation's status based on how quickly it was obtained.[^eth-code-p2][^eth-l1-p91][^eth-l1-p92][^eth-l1-p93]
