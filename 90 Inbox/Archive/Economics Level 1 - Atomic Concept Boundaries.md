# Economics Level 1 - Atomic Concept Boundaries

## Rationale

The deck is already a condensed review source. Named market structures, cost and revenue measures, cycle indicators, policy tools, geopolitical risks, trade mechanisms, and exchange-rate conventions therefore become canonical notes when they own a formula, distinct exam behavior, or a useful cross-topic destination. Existing nodes are enriched rather than duplicated.

## Proposed concepts by chapter

### Firms and Market Structures

Create or enrich: `Demand Function`, `Law of Demand`, `Demand Curve`, `Economic Profit`, `Accounting Profit`, `Normal Profit`, `Explicit Cost`, `Implicit Cost`, [[Opportunity Cost]], `Total Revenue`, `Average Revenue`, `Marginal Revenue`, `Total Cost`, `Fixed Cost`, `Variable Cost`, `Average Total Cost`, `Average Variable Cost`, `Average Fixed Cost`, `Marginal Cost`, [[Economies of Scale]], `Diseconomies of Scale`, `Minimum Efficient Scale`, `Profit Maximization`, [[Break-Even Point]], `Short-Run Shutdown Point`, `Long-Run Shutdown Point`, `Firm Supply Curve`, `Perfect Competition`, `Monopolistic Competition`, `Oligopoly`, `Monopoly`, [[Industry Concentration]], `N-Firm Concentration Ratio`, [[Herfindahl-Hirschman Index]], `Kinked Demand Curve`, `Nash Equilibrium`, `Cournot Model`, `Stackelberg Model`, and `Dominant Firm Model`.[^eco-boundaries-p4][^eco-boundaries-p16]

Cost measures remain atomic because their curve relationships and decision uses differ. The four market structures remain separate navigation targets, while shared comparison dimensions belong in the chapter map and contextual links.

### Business Cycles

Create or enrich: `Classical Business Cycle`, `Growth Cycle`, `Growth Rate Cycle`, `Business Cycle Phase`, `Output Gap`, `Consumer Confidence`, `Durable Goods Spending`, `Inventory-to-Sales Ratio`, `Credit Cycle`, `Composite Economic Indicator`, `Diffusion Index`, `Nowcasting`, `Leading Economic Indicator`, `Coincident Economic Indicator`, and `Lagging Economic Indicator`.[^eco-boundaries-p20][^eco-boundaries-p24]

Cycle definitions and indicator timing remain distinct because their peaks, troughs, and interpretation differ. Credit Cycle should connect directly to [[Fixed Income]], [[Credit Risk]], and liquidity analysis.

### Fiscal and Monetary Policy

Create or enrich: `Fiscal Policy`, `Monetary Policy`, `Fiscal Policy Objective`, `Government Spending`, `Transfer Payment`, `Capital Expenditure`, `Direct Tax`, `Indirect Tax`, `Keynesian Economics`, `Monetarism`, `Neoclassical Economics`, `Budget Surplus`, `Budget Deficit`, `Government Debt`, `Structural Budget Deficit`, `Cyclical Budget Deficit`, `Expansionary Fiscal Policy`, `Contractionary Fiscal Policy`, `Automatic Stabilizer`, `Discretionary Fiscal Policy`, `Recognition Lag`, `Action Lag`, `Impact Lag`, `Crowding Out`, `Central Bank`, `Inflation Targeting`, `Exchange Rate Targeting`, `Open Market Operations`, `Policy Rate`, `Repurchase Agreement`, `Federal Funds Rate`, `Reserve Requirement`, `Money Multiplier`, `Neutral Interest Rate`, `Expansionary Monetary Policy`, `Contractionary Monetary Policy`, `Demand-Pull Inflation`, `Cost-Push Inflation`, `Bond Market Vigilante`, `Liquidity Trap`, `Quantitative Easing`, and `Fiscal-Monetary Policy Mix`.[^eco-boundaries-p27][^eco-boundaries-p37]

Government debt must reuse or connect to the existing sovereign-credit framework rather than becoming an isolated macro node. Repurchase Agreement should resolve against any existing fixed-income repo note before creation.

### Geopolitics

Create or enrich: `Geopolitics`, `Geopolitical Actor`, `State Actor`, `Non-State Actor`, `Political Cooperation`, `Political Non-Cooperation`, `National Interest`, `Globalization`, `Nationalism`, `Soft Power`, `Hard Power`, `Geopolitical Tool`, `International Monetary Fund`, `World Bank Group`, `World Trade Organization`, `Geopolitical Risk`, `Geopolitical Event Risk`, `Exogenous Geopolitical Risk`, `Thematic Geopolitical Risk`, `Geopolitical Risk Assessment`, `Scenario Building`, `Signposting`, and `Flight to Quality`.[^eco-boundaries-p41][^eco-boundaries-p47]

The three geopolitical-risk classes remain atomic because their timing and evolution differ. Scenario building and signposting should link to the existing [[Scenario Analysis]] framework where appropriate without collapsing the geopolitical application into a generic statistics note.

### International Trade

Create or enrich: `International Trade`, `Comparative Advantage`, `Absolute Advantage`, `Tariff`, `Import Quota`, `Voluntary Export Restraint`, `Export Subsidy`, `Trade Restriction Welfare Effect`, `Free Trade Area`, `Customs Union`, `Common Market`, `Economic Union`, `Monetary Union`, `Trade Creation`, and `Trade Diversion`.[^eco-boundaries-p50][^eco-boundaries-p53]

Regional-integration forms remain separate because each adds a specific layer of shared policy or factor mobility. Trade creation and diversion must remain distinct welfare effects.

### FX Markets and Exchange Rates

Create or enrich: `Foreign Exchange Market`, `FX Spot Transaction`, [[Foreign Exchange Forward]], `FX Swap`, [[Hedging]], [[Speculation]], `Exchange Rate Regime`, `Fixed Exchange Rate`, `Floating Exchange Rate`, `Currency Board`, `Dollarization`, `Impossible Trinity`, `Trade Balance`, `Capital Account`, `Capital Control`, `Foreign Exchange Rate`, `Price Currency`, `Base Currency`, `Direct Exchange Rate Quotation`, `Indirect Exchange Rate Quotation`, `Nominal Exchange Rate`, `Real Exchange Rate`, `Bid Price`, `Offer Price`, [[Bid-Ask Spread]], `Currency Appreciation`, `Currency Depreciation`, `Cross Exchange Rate`, `Triangular Arbitrage`, `Forward Premium`, `Forward Discount`, `Forward Points`, and `Covered Interest Rate Parity`.[^eco-boundaries-p56][^eco-boundaries-p62]

`FX`, `SWF`, `IMF`, `WTO`, `FTA`, `HHI`, and policy abbreviations belong in aliases rather than filenames. `Foreign Exchange Forward` is enriched from the Derivatives source, not duplicated.

## Alias and cross-topic policy

- `MR`, `MC`, `ATC`, `AVC`, `AFC`, `MES`, `HHI`, `GDP gap`, `QE`, `OMO`, `IMF`, `WTO`, `FTA`, `FX`, `SWF`, and relevant Chinese translations become aliases only after normalized collision validation.
- `Repo` resolves to the canonical repurchase-agreement concept; `Bid-Offer Spread` resolves to [[Bid-Ask Spread]].
- Existing economics-adjacent equity, credit, fixed-income, quantitative, and derivative concepts receive Economics context and citations rather than duplicate files.
- Cross-topic links should connect market structure to [[Equity Investments]], cycles and policy to [[Fixed Income]] and [[Portfolio Management]], quantitative indicators to [[Quantitative Methods]], and FX forwards/parity to [[Derivatives]].
